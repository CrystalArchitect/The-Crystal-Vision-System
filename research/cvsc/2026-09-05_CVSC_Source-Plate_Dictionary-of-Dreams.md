### Description (as marketed / as catalogued)

Practical illustrated dream-interpretation reference. Classic texts plus an updated A–Z dictionary. Expanded and modernized from Miller 1901; contemporary additions include cell phones, computers, televisions. Marketing claims coverage of more than 15,000 dream symbols. This is not a pure reprint of Miller.
