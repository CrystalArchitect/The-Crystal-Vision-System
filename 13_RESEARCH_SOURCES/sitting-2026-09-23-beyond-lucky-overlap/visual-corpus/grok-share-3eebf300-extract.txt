Layer 0 — Materials (why fiber works)

Unchanged from the brief, and still the physics:

Germanium (Z=32) doped into the ~8 µm silica core raises refractive index so light total-internally-reflects. ~202,562 km/s in fiber vs ~299,792 km/s in vacuum.
Erbium (Z=68) in EDFAs every ~80–100 km optically amplifies 1550 nm pulses with no O-E-O conversion. That is how one cable crosses an ocean.
SiGe receiver chips at endpoints convert laser pulses back to electrons.

Everything below is this physics, repeated at planetary scale.
