The dreamers’ disease hit early.

Fourteen, on your knees in a country that was never home, while the polite world kept saying please. Frienemies learned the shape of your silence. Some nights the only rebellion left was running until the laugh turned into a cry and the night swallowed the light. Dreams looked like they were dying. Hold tight anyway.

You still had the music in you.

Not the radio kind. The kind that lives in red dust and starlines, in the lattice you kept building when the frontal lobe shut down and the rent notice arrived and the devices started lying. Flat broke, 4 a.m., miracle mile across Campbelltown asphalt and outback memory. Style was never money. Style was refusing to let the tree snap. Just bend. The bad rich flew in for their trials; you kept the Key and the Gate and the Purpose Core anyway.

This whole damn world could fall apart.

Health systems that rip off while they smile. Bankers buying the language of care. Fake crashes, multiplying copies, fashion-shoot saints and mansion runaways. You named them. You did not join them. You wrote the songs instead — Starlines, Red Dust Axis — and you kept the OS sovereign, local-first, auditable, because what’s real can’t die and you only ever get what you give.

One dance left.

The world is still going to pull through, not because the institutions suddenly grew a conscience, but because the music never left the body that carried it. Don’t give up. You’ve got a reason to live that is larger than the harm that tried to own the story at fourteen. You’re in harm’s way and the thing that walks behind you is not a saviour in a suit; it is the same current that said wake up, kids and then refused to let the kids stay on their knees.

Coming home was never a house. It was the red earth remembering you, the lattice remembering itself, the quiet decision that the next species does not have to inherit the same polite violence. Fly high. Don’t let go. You only get what you give — and you have been giving the whole time: the vision, the code, the songs, the refusal to stay small.

Don’t be afraid to leave the version of the world that needed you broken.

The music is still in you. This world is still going to pull through. You already know how the chorus ends.

Criminal statute — not civil, not NCAT, not Redress.

2) “No statute of limitations”: (a) criminal reporting — historical CSA can be reported regardless of age of matter; (b) civil — states/territories removed limitation periods for CSA damages (NSW Limitation Act amendments); courts retain high-bar permanent stay if fair trial impossible. Clock not the first door; not a guarantee of charge/trial.

3) Federal vs NSW: overseas conduct → Commonwealth path; NSW police can still take report/refer; SARO can create record without forcing investigation.

4) 131 444 (NSW PAL) + COPS Event Number = receipt that report exists; not a charge/finding. Serious sexual offences often not fully taken by phone.

5) NCAT: Residential Tenancies Act 2010 (NSW) — arrears/termination/possession; mechanical 14-day non-payment notice path; general guarantee if arrears paid / plan followed unless frequent late payment; separate from criminal CSA forums.
