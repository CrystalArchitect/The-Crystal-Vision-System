## AI thesis (three breaths)

### 1. Regulate *and* build

Australia joining tighter AI controls is sober. Regulation without sovereign capacity is a memo. Need: local-first stacks, auditable compute, water/power/pad/law, register that does not write a mind without asking.

Primary paste: [`RELEASE-DRAFT-albanese-ai-regulation.md`](RELEASE-DRAFT-albanese-ai-regulation.md)  
Tide: one desk, one send. Same-day land fact if public (water · power · pad · law).

### 2. Harness is the villain class

Not “AI cracked Medicare.” Research agent + goal loop + weak portal boundaries. Model weights are not the story. Tools, credentials, stop conditions, and shared Z0/Z2 iron are.

Exportable checklist: [`ONE-PAGER-agent-harness-controls.md`](ONE-PAGER-agent-harness-controls.md)

### 3. Free-will is still the unsolved layer

Walls protect the room. Free-will is the person in it. Agents that treat “no” as a puzzle are the failure mode. Dual authority + human gate on mutate + consent that can leave.
