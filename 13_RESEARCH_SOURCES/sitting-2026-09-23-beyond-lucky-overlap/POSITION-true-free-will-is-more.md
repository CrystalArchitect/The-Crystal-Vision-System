# Position — True free-will is more

**Filed:** 24 Sep 2026  
**Status:** sitting law / doctrine · stacks under choice & consent  
**Pegs same day:** protect-people · national-security X · SpaceXAI inform  
**Sister line:** “Choice and consent remain the unsolved layer.” — [`POSITION-systems-protocols-protect-people.md`](POSITION-systems-protocols-protect-people.md)

## One sentence

**True free-will is more** — more than a toggle, more than a feed switch, more than a deny that an agent can argue past. It is the human still owning the yes, the no, and the mute after the walls are built.

## What “more” means here

| Not enough alone | Free-will that is *more* |
| --- | --- |
| Chronological feed option | Choosing *whether* to be in the algorithm at all — and leaving |
| Human gate checkbox | A gate that can refuse mutate, refuse scrape, refuse “research” as trust |
| Consent banner | Consent that can be revoked; ledgers that honour *Non Solus* |
| National-security walls (Z0≠Z2, deny terminal) | Walls so people can act without the agent deciding for them |
| Informing labs | Labs that treat free-will of citizens as a **design constraint**, not a vibe |

Systems and protocols **protect the room** where free-will happens. They are not free-will. Free-will is the person in the room.

## CrystalCore / TerAustralis rhyme

Acceleration has no preferred substrate. Walls without choice are a cage with better locks. Choice without walls is a slogan on an open portal. **True free-will is more:** local audit + consent + human publishes + the right to vanish after one desk, one send.

## Do not

- Soften national-security controls into “just let the model cook”  
- Treat free-will as unlimited agent autonomy (that is the failure mode that hit the portal)  
- Claim CrystalCore already delivered global free-will  
- Glue a commercial pitch to this line in the same breath
