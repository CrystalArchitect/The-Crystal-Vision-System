## What it is (fact frame)

Google + DeepMind (+ UMD / UVA) framework for **recursive self-improvement at the exploration-policy layer**, not weight updates.

Loop:
1. **Online explore** — current policy drives a coding agent; logs a discovery tree  
2. **Build replay simulator** — past trees become a cheap world  
3. **Dream** — LLM rewrites / scores alternate exploration policies *inside* that history  
4. Redeploy winning policy → new trees → pool grows  

**Fixed:** model weights, evaluator, tools. **What improves:** the exploration-policy program.

Reported efficiency (paper claims; **single-lab preprint / experiment frame**): up to ~162× fewer discovery calls vs SimpleTES on one Lasso task; ~1.7× vs fixed exploration; large budget cuts on some math tasks. Treat as **lab experiment**, not shipped capability.
