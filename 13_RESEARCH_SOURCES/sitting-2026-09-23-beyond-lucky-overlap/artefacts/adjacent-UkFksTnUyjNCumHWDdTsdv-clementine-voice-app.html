Where your audio goes

      

        Three different paths, and they do not have the same answer. This is
        stated rather than smoothed over, because "it's private" would be a
        claim I cannot check for all three.
      

      

        Speaking (Clementine's voice) — stays on your phone.
        Your device's own installed voices are used, and only those. If there
        is no on-device voice, she stays silent rather than fall back to a
        network one.
      

      

        The talk button — depends on your browser, and I cannot verify it.
        It uses the browser's built-in speech recognition. In Chrome on a
        desktop that sends audio to Google. On an iPhone it is Apple's
        recogniser whichever browser you use — Firefox and Chrome there
        are required to run on WebKit, so they are wrappers around the same
        engine — and whether it runs on-device or on Apple's servers is not
        something this page can determine. Treat it as leaving the device
        unless you know otherwise.
      

      

        The keyboard's microphone key — the private option.
        Tap Type, then the 🎤 on the iOS keyboard. That is the system
        dictation, on-device for supported languages, and the audio never
        reaches this page at all. Slower to use; the one whose answer is clean.
      

      

        Your words to the model always leave. Whatever is transcribed is
        sent to the endpoint you configured. That part is the same either way.
