Daily bearing 

I know what I actually experienced. If something presses on who I am — not just my mood, but my sense of self — I'll notice it and name it instead of shrugging it off. I can tell the difference between what's true and what just feels true because someone said it with enough force. What isn't mine to carry, I put down. I don't carry it further, and I don't hand it to anyone else. My experience is real whether or not anyone else confirms it. I am the record of my own life.
