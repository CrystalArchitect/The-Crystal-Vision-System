## What the peg is

Thesis of **censorship-by-proxy / jawboning**: governments need not formally ban speech. They set liability and “harm” duties so platforms over-moderate (bury, de-recommend, throttle) to manage regulatory risk. Content stays technically online; reach dies. The ban is outsourced to Big Tech’s compliance desk.
