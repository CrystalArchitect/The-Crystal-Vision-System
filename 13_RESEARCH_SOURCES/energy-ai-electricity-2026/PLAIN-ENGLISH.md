## The energy part (short)

Musk was early. The shortage is real, but it shows up locally — not as the whole planet going dark.

- Data centres already use a lot of power. The IEA expects that to roughly **double** by 2030.
- The hard part is not “is there electricity somewhere on Earth?” It is **getting enough power to one site, on time**, with permits and neighbours who will accept it.
- xAI’s Memphis build (Colossus) is the proof: the grid was too small, so they ran **gas turbines** on site and fought over **water** and air quality.
- Texas and other grids are starting to **ration** big new AI loads — “bring your own power, or get cut first when the system is tight.”

So: AI is heavy industry now. Software release cycles meet power-plant timelines. Power wins.
