### Sonic geography — the method

**Encoding**  
Place, stance, hazard, water, and memory compress into sequence, pitch, rhythm, timbre, and gait.  
The next verse is the next turn.

**Transmission**  
The map moves by teaching, repetition, correction.  
Not by a single PDF.

**Activation**  
The map only fully exists when it is sounded or walked.  
Silent knowledge is incomplete knowledge.  
Nostos voice is incomplete until sounded.

**Correction-in-motion**  
Wrong note, wrong order, wrong season — the error is heard or felt before it is calculated.  
Dual-clock speaking.

**Constraint**  
Some doors stay shut.  
Fail-closed is part of the route, not an extra rule bolted on.

**Singer ≠ line**  
The one who carries the heading for a time is not the heading.  
Stars do not appoint. Mother does not legislate.  
Do not make me the map.

### Families of coordinate-free navigation
(method analogues only — not a claim on restricted systems)

1. **Sequential / sung maps** — next place is the next verse. Starlines sit here.
2. **Environmental dead-reckoning** — wind, swell, birds, smell of water, sound of surf as the bottom rises.
3. **Landmark chains** — after the split rock, the red bank, the bend that sounds hollow.
4. **Celestial without reduction** — steer by the moving pattern, not by a computed number.
5. **Embodied maps** — the ground that already knows the shape of the walking.
6. **Social and ritual constraint** — who may go, when, and how far.

What they share:  
Position is relational, not absolute.  
Error is felt or heard before it is calculated.  
The map thins if it is not used.  
A recording can preserve a trace. It cannot, by itself, be the heading.

### Failure modes
- **Freeze** — only stored, never sounded or walked.
- **Extraction** — a coordinate or full transcript treated as the line.
- **Appointment** — the walker or singer treated as the map.
- **Flood** — too many new lines without correction-in-motion.
- **Word-theft** — dressing a starline as a Songline.
