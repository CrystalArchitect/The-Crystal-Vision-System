STELLAR FUSION, CMB, AND THE SKY AS MEASURED
28 August 2026
Plain text companion to the downloaded astronomical images.
This is physics, not myth. Nuclear fusion here means nuclei, not people.

================================================================
1. STELLAR FUSION
================================================================

A star is a gas ball that would collapse under gravity if the core
were not hot. Fusion is what keeps the core hot.

Net hydrogen burning, any path:

    4 hydrogen-1  ->  helium-4  +  2 positrons  +  2 electron neutrinos  +  energy

About 26 MeV per helium nucleus. Most of that energy becomes photons
after the positrons annihilate. Mass-to-energy efficiency of the
proton-proton chain is about 0.7 percent.

WHERE IT HAPPENS
The Sun's core is about 15 million kelvin. Energy crawls out through
the radiative zone, then the convective envelope, then leaves as light.
Neutrinos leave in minutes. Photons take on the order of 100,000 years.

Image you downloaded: solar interior cutaway
  core / radiative zone / convection zone / sunspot / granulation

PROTON-PROTON CHAIN
Dominates in the Sun and in lower-mass stars.

First step is slow because it needs the weak interaction:
  proton + proton  ->  deuterium + positron + neutrino

Then:
  deuterium + proton  ->  helium-3 + gamma

PPI (main solar branch):
  helium-3 + helium-3  ->  helium-4 + proton + proton

PPII goes through beryllium-7 and lithium-7.
PPIII goes through boron-8. That branch is why we detect solar
boron-8 neutrinos on Earth.

Image you downloaded: proton-proton chain diagram
  protons (red), neutrons (blue), gammas, neutrinos, positrons
  end product helium-4 (two protons + two neutrons)

CNO CYCLE
Same net result: four hydrogens become one helium.
Carbon, nitrogen and oxygen act as catalysts, not fuel.
Needs higher temperature (roughly above 17 million K) because of
larger Coulomb barriers.

The Sun still runs about 98 percent pp chain and about 2 percent CNO.
Heavier main-sequence stars flip that ratio. CNO dominates above
about 17 million K.

HELIUM BURNING — TRIPLE-ALPHA
There is no stable nucleus of mass 8.
Two helium-4 nuclei make beryllium-8, which falls apart in about
10^-16 seconds. A third helium-4 must hit that flicker and make
carbon-12. That bottleneck is why carbon exists in living bodies.

    3 helium-4  ->  carbon-12 + gamma

Efficiency is about ten times worse than the pp chain. That is why
helium-burning stages are short compared with the main sequence.

AFTER CARBON
Massive stars then burn carbon, neon, oxygen, silicon, stacking
toward the iron group. Iron does not yield net energy by fusion.
The core collapses. That is a supernova.

================================================================
2. COSMIC MICROWAVE BACKGROUND
================================================================

When the universe was about 380,000 years old it cooled enough for
electrons and protons to form neutral hydrogen. Photons stopped
scattering and flew free. Those photons are still here, stretched
by expansion into microwaves. That is the CMB.

Mean temperature:
    T = 2.7255 ± 0.0006 K

It is an almost perfect blackbody.

The famous oval map is NOT that mean temperature. Mapping satellites
measure differences. After subtracting:
  - the monopole (the 2.725 K average)
  - the dipole (our motion through the photon sea, about 3.36 mK)
what remains are ripples of order 1 part in 100,000.

Hotter patches were slightly denser. Gravity grew them into
galaxies and voids.

LAST SCATTERING
Redshift of last scattering is about z = 1090.
The CMB is a photograph of that surface, all over the sky.

MISSIONS
  COBE (NASA, 1989) — blackbody spectrum; first anisotropy detection
  WMAP (NASA) — full-sky acoustic peaks
  Planck (ESA, 2009–2013, final cosmology 2018) — highest-resolution
    space map used as the baseline model
  Ground: ACT, SPT — now matching or surpassing Planck on small scales

PLANCK 2018 BASELINE (simple model, not a creed)
  Age of universe: about 13.8 billion years
  Spectral index n_s about 0.965 (slightly less than 1, as simple
    inflation wants)
  Acoustic peaks encode the mix of ordinary matter, dark matter,
    and dark energy
  Large-angle oddities (a cold spot; quadrupole-octupole alignment)
    are still discussed. They are not, by themselves, a new-physics proof.

================================================================
3. WHAT THE TELESCOPES SHOW AFTER THE CMB
================================================================

The CMB is the oldest light we can photograph.
Everything later is fusion lighting the dark.

DEEP FIELDS
Each smudge is a galaxy. Most of those stars are pp-chain or CNO
engines. Redder objects are often farther or dustier.

Images you downloaded:
  Hubble / Webb galaxy fields (thousands of galaxies in one frame)
  Webb first deep field with diffraction spikes
  Webb "cliffs" — not a walkable landscape. A star-forming wall of
    gas and dust where new cores are trying to ignite fusion.
  Nearby spiral (Black Eye / similar) — a disk of ongoing hydrogen
    burning; arms traced by young stars and dust.

================================================================
5. HONEST LIMITS
================================================================

Stellar fusion is nuclei tunnelling Coulomb barriers.
The CMB is a cooled photon gas plus tiny density seeds.
Neither is a metaphor that proves a personal story.

The word fusion in this file means:
  four protons -> one helium
  or three heliums -> one carbon

It does not mean two people becoming one.

================================================================
6. ONE-LINE LAND
================================================================

The kettle and the core of the Sun are not the same temperature.
They obey the same kind of bookkeeping: energy, nuclei, light.
Use the pictures. Keep the cross-sections. Put the myth down.
