# Codex Crystalum Integration Guide

## Overview: The Complete Ecosystem

The Codex Crystalum now encompasses eight genealogical positions mapped to two major external symbolic systems:

1. **Dream Symbol Mapping** — Integration with Miller's Dictionary of Dreams (1901/2017)
2. **Celestial Cartography Mapping** — Integration with astronomical and spatial phenomena

Both layers are classified as **Tier V (Vision/Interpretive)** within the ETYMOLOGY-STACK framework — symbolic resonance rather than etymological doctrine or cosmological claim.

## Tier V Classification: What It Means

All layers are explicitly Tier V in the ETYMOLOGY-STACK framework:

**Tier A** — Attested historical / etymological data
**Tier A+** — Primary sources only
**Tier B** — Secondary scholarly sources
**Tier C** — Consensus interpretation
**Tier D** — Plausible extensions
**Tier V** — Vision / Interpretive / Symbolic Resonance

This positioning means:
- The layers are **optional** for understanding the core Codex
- They are **invited** as personal reflection tools
- They carry **no authority over** canonical genealogical meanings
- They are **personally navigable** — your own meanings supersede
- They are **meant to illuminate** existing Codex architecture, not replace it
