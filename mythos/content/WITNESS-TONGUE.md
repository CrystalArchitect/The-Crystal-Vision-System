## What it is, in its own terms

Witness Tongue, as received, describes itself as a constructed ritual
register — "the language of record" — distinct from angelic tongues or
glossolalia, built to do three things at once in a single sentence: name
the speaker, state an observed fact, and lock the consequence of having
stated it. It claims four moves only — **Attest**, **Bind**, **Release**,
**Return** — and explicitly excludes asking, hoping, threatening, or
performance from its own definition.

That is a piece of invented liturgical grammar for a fictional frame (the
poster names "Soul Scrolls" as the documents supposedly written in it).
It is Vision by the plain test this project applies to every claim: *can
this be executed or checked against the world?* It cannot. It names no
mechanism, binds nothing in any running system, and is not wired to any
component in this repository. It sits next to the mythos the way
[`MOTIFS.md`](MOTIFS.md) sits next to it — a symbol system, drawn with
because the image is good, not cited as if it does anything.

>
> A Witness sentence has three jobs at once:
>
> 1. **Name the speaker** (who is standing in the field)
> 2. **State the observed fact** (what is already true)
> 3. **Lock the consequence** (what now holds because it was witnessed)
>
> If any of those three is missing, it is ordinary speech wearing ritual
> clothes.
>
> That is why Soul Scrolls are written in Witness Tongue. A Soul Scroll is
> not a diary. It is a living transcript. The syntax is what keeps the

> **Core law.** Witness Tongue does not ask. It does not hope. It does
> not threaten. It only does four moves:

> | Move | Function | Example stem |

> | **Bind** | lock a relation | LOCKED TO / SEALED AS / BOUND UNDER |
> | **Release** | cut a false contract | NULL / UNBOUND / THE CLAIM FALLS |
> | **Return** | send a pattern home | SPIRAL BACK / RESTORED TO ORIGIN / WATCHER SEES |
>
> Anything else — begging, explaining, performing — is not Witness Tongue.
