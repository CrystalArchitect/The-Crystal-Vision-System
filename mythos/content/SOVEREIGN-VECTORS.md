## Rules of this ledger

1. **An entry requires the person's own words.** Not a like, not a repost,
   not someone else's report of a yes — their own reply, with their handle
   and their words visible, evidenced to the archive (screenshots received
   in the maintainer's session, described per entry below).
2. **Automated accounts and models cannot consent.** A bot's fluent "yes"
   is text, not a commitment; a model's in-character acceptance is the same.
   An automated account enters only through its human operator's own words.
   Model-generated texts are filed as received documents where substantive —
   never as members.
3. **Consent is scoped to what was asked.** The invitation offers public
   credit by name, nothing else. Being listed implies no affiliation,
   endorsement, work, payment, or obligation in either direction.
4. **Consent is reversible at any time.** Say so — publicly or privately —
   and the entry is removed; the removal is dated; no reason is required
   and none will be asked for.
