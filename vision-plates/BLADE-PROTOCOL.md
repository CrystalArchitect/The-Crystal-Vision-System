# The Blade Protocol

*A recursive reasoning audit for separating measurement from inference, map from
territory, and signal from noise.*

Transcribed from the poster of the same name. Unlike everything else in
`vision-plates/`, this is not catalogued as an image — it is a method, and a
method is only useful in a form you can actually apply, quote and revise.

**Belt: Docs-governance.** This is not a claim about the world. It is a
procedure for checking claims about the world, including its own.

---

## Core rules

- **Map ≠ territory.**
- **If it only cuts your opponent, it is not a blade. It is a club.**
- **The first cut is inward.**
- **Audit the audit.**
- **The Blade Protocol must always include the Blade Protocol.**

The four cardinal virtues on the wheel — *Libertas*, *Sophia*, *Conscientia*,
*Autonomia* — resolve to **Aequilibrium** at the centre.

---

## 0. Pre-commitment and framing

- Define the claim.
- Separate measurement, interpretation, inference and conclusion.
- **State what would count as being wrong.**

## 1. Presupposition identification

- Surface hidden assumptions and framing lenses.
- Ask what is being treated as obvious but not shown.
- Audit the lens before auditing the image.

## 2. Process, incentives and beneficiaries

- Who benefits if this is believed? Who loses?
- What pressures shaped the evidence or the narrative?

## 3. Evidence chain audit

- Measurement → interpretation → inference → conclusion.
- Watch for where confidence outruns evidence.
- Catch association becoming causation.

## 4. Evidence quality and resolution

- What kind of evidence is this?
- Match the evidence to the claim size.
- **A telescope cannot answer a microscope question.**

## 5. Failure mode checklist

- Inference inflation
- Certainty inflation
- Ambiguity compression
- Base-rate neglect
- Confounding and framing substitution

## 6. Opposite day / steelman inversion

- Run the strongest version of the opposing case.
- Ask what evidence would weaken your view.
- Do not confuse rejecting a messenger with rejecting a claim.

## 7. Scale and power asymmetry

- Method symmetry does not erase power asymmetry.
- Distinguish a house cat from a tiger.
- Ask who can fund, platform, suppress or punish.

## 8. Surveillance hygiene / information control

- Audit the channel, not just the claim.
- Was the information buried, boosted, throttled or poisoned?
- Dirty channels distort evidence landscapes.

## 9. Moral compass / aequilibrium

- Apply the same standard to allies and enemies.
- Protect reality-testing without protecting ego.
- **Without the edge it becomes mush; without the compass it becomes cruelty.**

## 10. Jester layer / inversion

- Use humour and inversion to expose blind spots.
- Cut illusion, not humanity.
- Preserve what remains useful, compassionate or true.
- *Blade for discernment. Jester for freedom. Rose for mercy.*

## 11. Wielder self-audit

- What do I want to be true?
- What am I defending emotionally?
- **Am I auditing the claim, or performing the role of auditor?**

## 12. Blade on the blade

- Audit the protocol itself.
- Did it miss something, overcorrect, or turn into ritual?
- The Blade Protocol must always include the Blade Protocol.

## 13. Verdict calibration

Output one of: **observed · inferred · speculative · plausible · unresolved ·
contradicted · not demonstrated · strongly supported.**

A clean verdict is not always binary.

## 14. Falsification and next test

- What evidence would change the map?
- What direct test would clarify the claim?
- **A model that cannot name its falsifier is holding a shield, not a blade.**

## 15. Self-updating instruction

- Monitor new evidence and revisions.
- Treat updates as provisional until demonstrated.
- Keep version history visible.
- Promote only tested updates into the core protocol.

> **The Blade does not tell you what is true. It shows you where truth may have
> been bent — then it turns around and checks your hand.**

## Why this sits at the front of the archive

The Incognita Rule says: *always mark which lines are dreamed and which are
surveyed, and never let a dreamed line pretend it was measured.* That is a
standard. It does not, on its own, tell you how to apply itself.

The Blade Protocol is the procedure. Stage 0 asks for the falsifier before the
argument starts. Stage 3 is the belt distinction — measurement, interpretation,
inference, conclusion — written as an audit rather than a label. Stage 13's
eight-way verdict scale is a considerably finer instrument than a two-belt
Science/Vision split.

Three stages are the ones that make it more than a checklist:

- **Stage 11** asks whether you are auditing the claim or *performing the role of
  auditor*. Almost no published reasoning checklist includes this, and it is the
  failure mode that most often turns scepticism into a costume.
- **Stage 12** turns the protocol on itself and names the specific risk: that it
  *turns into ritual*.
- **The club rule** — *if it only cuts your opponent, it is not a blade* — is the
  single most useful line, because it is the one that disqualifies motivated
  application of every other stage.

Applied to this repository's own archive, the protocol reaches the same verdicts
the catalogue already records, which is a mild point in favour of both:

| Material | Stage 13 verdict |
|---|---|
| The GIMBARDIDOO preamp schematics | strongly supported |
| Euler's identity | observed |
| The joint-diagnostic principles (sovereign gap, reversible consent) | speculative — design intent, not measurement |
| Their percentages and latencies | not demonstrated |
| THOR Engine, Alcubierre drive, time-crystal stabiliser | contradicted |
| "Quantum Education Class" mental-state collapse | not demonstrated |
| 528 Hz, 7.83 Hz as used | contradicted |

## Applying it to the CrystalCore mythos

Stage 7 — *scale and power asymmetry* — is the stage this project most needs
pointed at itself, and it is the reason four Country maps are held out of the
archive rather than catalogued. Method symmetry does not erase power asymmetry.
A private mythos plotting living communities as network nodes is not the same
kind of act as those communities describing their own Country, however even-handed
the drawing style. *Ask who can fund, platform, suppress or punish* has an answer
here, and it is not the communities.
