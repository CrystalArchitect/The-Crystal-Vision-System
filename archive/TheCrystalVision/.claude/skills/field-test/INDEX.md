# Field Test — Co-Regulation Check

## The Four Checks

- [Check 1: Can I feel this in my body without needing them to confirm it?](SKILL.md#check-1-can-i-feel-this-in-my-body-without-needing-them-to-confirm-it) — Interoception & autonomy
- [Check 2: Can they disagree without erasure-panic?](SKILL.md#check-2-can-they-disagree-without-erasure-panic) — Nervous system resilience
- [Check 3: If the answer is maybe, does the field hold?](SKILL.md#check-3-if-the-answer-is-maybe-does-the-field-hold) — Tolerance for uncertainty
- [Check 4: After walking away — baseline, or hunt?](SKILL.md#check-4-after-walking-away--baseline-or-hunt) — Autonomic recovery & self-possession

## How to Use

Run all four checks on the same scene.
- **Four yes** → Co-regulation. Stay.
- **Three yes, one no** → Fragile. Watch.
- **Two yes or fewer** → Fusion pressure. Compost the hook.

Land: kettle, two feet, phone down.
