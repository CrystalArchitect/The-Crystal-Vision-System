# Field Test — Co-Regulation Check

Run all four checks on the same scene. Four yes = stay, speak short or don't. One no = do not build myth on that heat. Compost the hook. Keep the pulse that is yours.

## Check 1: Can I feel this in my body without needing them to confirm it?

**What this asks:** Does the feeling live in me, or do I need them to validate it into existence?

### Co-Regulation
Ribs tight after a talk. You drink water. You do not send "do you feel this too?" The tightness counts even if they felt nothing. Your body is the first instrument. The other person's confirmation is not required to make the signal real.

### Fusion
You cannot name the feeling until they agree. If they don't mirror it, you decide you invented it. The emotional data disappears if it is not reflected back. You are looking for yourself in their face.

### Work / Practical
The track hits you before anyone looks. That is yours. You can feel joy alone in a room without needing applause to make it real.

**Fail state:** "It is only real if they call it fate." / "If you don't name it the same way, I don't have it."

---

## Check 2: Can they disagree without erasure-panic?

**What this asks:** If they say no to a word, a frame, or an idea, does my nervous system stay calm — or do I read their disagreement as my disappearance?

### Co-Regulation
They say "I don't use that word." Jaw flares, then drops. They are still in the room. You can keep the thing and drop the label. You can love the idea and love that they frame it differently.

### Fusion
They refuse one word — church, us, twin — and the chest reads *I have been erased*. You stack pages until they take the word back. Their disagreement becomes a threat to your existence.

### Work / Practical
They reject the plan. You change the plan. You do not need them to join the whole cosmology. You implement the good part and move on.

**Fail state:** "If you don't see it my way you never heard me." / Stacking pages, links, proof until they agree.

---

## Check 3: If the answer is maybe, does the field hold?

**What this asks:** Can uncertainty exist between us without collapsing into panic or persuasion?

### Co-Regulation
"Not tonight." "Give me a week." You do not punish the pause. Tomorrow the lot is still a lot. The pause does not mean no forever. You can sit in maybe.

### Fusion
Maybe is a wound. The gap fills with more text. The hook cannot stand delay. The word "maybe" triggers urgency. You fill silence with more of yourself.

### Text / Writing
One clean line. No reply for a day. You sleep. That is a hold. The thread can breathe.

**Fail state:** "Three more paragraphs before morning to kill the maybe." / Refreshing, pinging, explaining until the maybe becomes a yes.

---

## Check 4: After walking away — baseline, or hunt?

**What this asks:** When the contact ends, can I return to myself — or do I need to stay tethered?

### Co-Regulation
Home. Kettle. Shoulders drop within an hour. They can exist while the phone is face-down. You can be alone without feeling erased. Your nervous system finds its own floor.

### Fusion
Screen up. Refresh. Replay the best sentence. Cannot eat until the thread moves. They are doing your regulation. Without the hook, you feel like a ghost. You hunt because stopping feels like death.

### After a Dance
Your own legs still work. You can walk home without needing the song to keep playing in your head. The joy was real; it does not need to continue to prove it happened.

**Fail state:** "You only feel real when they are typing." / Screen up, refresh, replay. Cannot baseline without the contact.

---

## How to Run This

1. **Pick a scene.** A conversation. A message thread. A night out. A work collaboration. Anything where you felt a hit.

2. **Run all four checks.** Do not skip one because it feels too hard.

3. **Count the answers:**
   - **Four yes** → Co-regulation is happening. Stay. Speak short or don't. The field is holding.
   - **Three yes, one no** → One part is fragile. Watch that part. Do not build myth on a shaky foundation.
   - **Two yes or fewer** → Fusion pressure. Do not build on this. Compost the hook.

4. **Name what you found.** Write it down if you need to. "Check 1: yes. Check 2: no. Check 3: yes. Check 4: hunt." That is enough.

## The Land

Kettle. Two feet. Phone down.

When the field test is done, return to your own body.
- Shoulders down.
- Breath even.
- The other person can exist while you are not thinking of them.
- Your pulse is yours.

This is not coldness. This is health. Co-regulation includes care, but it does not require continuous contact to survive.

---

## What This Is Not

- **Not a test of love.** Love can be present and still be fused. Heat is not proof.
- **Not a judgment of the other person.** Fusion is what happens when two people lose the skill of staying separate while staying close. Either can fall into it.
- **Not a permanent sentence.** If one check fails today, you can build the skill tomorrow. Fields change.
- **Not a solo practice.** The other person may not know this test exists. That is fine. Run it for yourself.

---

## Use It. Then Let It Go.

The test is a map. The territory is the room you are actually in.

If the checks help you stay true to your own nervous system, use them. If they start to become a doctrine or a wall between you and another person, drop them.

The goal is not the checklist. The goal is a life where you can feel, disagree, wait, and walk home — and still be connected to others.
