# Bergson’s filter vs the predictive brain (and the reducing valve)

*Working note · TerAustralis Incognita · 2026-09-04*  

---

## 1. Bergson’s claim (tight)

In *Matter and Memory* and the *Dreams* lecture, Bergson treats the brain less as a factory that *makes* consciousness and more as an organ of **attention to life**: it selects, inhibits, and orients so the organism can **act**.

- Waking: the valve is tight. Useful perception + habit-memory at the tip of the cone.
- Sleep / dream: “disinterestedness.” The filter loosens; pure memory presses up (steam in the boiler).
- Perception is for action; pure memory is not a pile of brain-stored files in his account — the body *calls* what it needs for the present plane.

So: **eliminative / selective function**, not only productive. The practical intellect spatializes time (clock, samples, labels) because action needs handles. Durée — lived continuous time — is what gets cut when we mistake the handle for the whole.

## 2. Huxley’s reducing valve (bridge, with a warning)

Aldous Huxley (*The Doors of Perception*, 1954) popularises Bergson as a **reducing valve**: Mind-at-Large narrowed to a “measly trickle” for biological usefulness; mescaline cracks the valve.

Useful culturally. Slightly dangerous philosophically. Scholars (e.g. Sjöstedt-Hughes) note Huxley simplifies Bergson: Bergson’s filter is tied to **action and durée**, not only to a cosmic firehose of raw mind. For Aerotropolis design ethics, keep Bergson’s precision: we are instrumenting **attention-to-life tension**, not promising portal tourism.

## 3. Predictive processing / free-energy (what labs actually speak)

Karl Friston’s **free-energy principle** and predictive processing: the brain maintains a hierarchical **generative model**; it minimises surprise / free energy by updating beliefs (perception) or acting on the world (active inference). High-level **priors** constrain lower-level sensory flux. Attention can be read as precision-weighting — which prediction errors get to matter.

This is the lingua franca of much contemporary theoretical neuroscience. It is **metaphysically thinner** than Bergson: usually physicalist, computational, no required “Mind-at-Large.”

## 4. Where they rhyme (use this in briefings)

| Bergson | Predictive / REBUS-adjacent |
| --- | --- |
| Attention to life = filter for action | Priors + precision = constraint for adaptive inference |
| Dreams / loosened interest → memory flood | Relaxed high-level priors (REBUS: Carhart-Harris & Friston on psychedelics) → bottom-up freer |
| Tip of cone = present body in action plane | Task BCI = decode actionable latent for control |
| Base of cone = deep past / pure memory | Autobiographical / state dynamics not reducible to one motor label |
| Intellect spatializes time | Sampling rate, epochs, discrete classes |

**REBUS** (Relaxed Beliefs Under Psychedelics) describes dynamics that *look like* a filter loosening, without endorsing Huxley’s ontology. For a dual-rail lab: Rail B can study **constraint relaxation** (priors, DMN, precision) as measurable correlates of Bergson’s “tension,” without smuggling Vision into Built.

## 5. Where they fight (do not paper over)

1. **Ontology.** Bergson: mind is not produced by the brain as a mere generator; brain selects for life. Predictive brain: mind as inference over a generative model implemented in tissue. Same *engineering* of gates; different story about what the gate opens onto.
2. **Time.** Durée vs clocked sampling. A classifier that only lives in windows will always be tip-tech. Claiming it “reads consciousness” commits the spatializing error Bergson named.
3. **Memory.** Habit vs pure memory. Motor-imagery BCI ≈ habit-memory interfaces. Dream / narrative / hypnagogic work ≈ approach to the base — needs different consent and metrics, not a bigger SVM.
4. **Political risk.** Filter language can be hijacked: “open the valve” as extractive productisation of altered states. Bergson’s ethic for a healthy participant: the person stays co-interpreter of their durée; the lab does not silently own the steam.

## 6. Design rule for Aerotropolis (operational)

- **Rail A** speaks Friston fluently: action, prediction error, control, dual-use receipts.
- **Rail B** speaks Bergson fluently: attention-to-life as tension; tip/base cone; no collapse of meaning into a label.
- **Shared hardware, split metaphysics in the consent form.** You may *use* predictive models without *believing* they exhaust consciousness.
- Success: not “we proved Mind-at-Large.” Success: Australian-sited capacity where **sensing the sky (DARC)** and **sensing ourselves in time (BCI)** stay coupled without one eating the other.

## 7. One sentence spine

**Predictive neuroscience tells you how the valve is engineered; Bergson tells you why a civilisation that only funds the valve — never the steam, never the participant — ends up with awareness on lease.**
