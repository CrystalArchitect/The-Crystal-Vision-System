# Celestial Atlas of the Southern Sky

**Vision-layer atlas series.** Gold on indigo. Charted from the Edge. Not a sky survey, not a star catalogue, and not an H2 result.

Observed from the Edge · Epochs 1857.3–1862.3 (atlas time). The numbers are plate fiction. They do not date any experiment.

## Interpretation Boundary

This series does **not** demonstrate:

- Extra-spatial or multi-dimensional compute (Plate XXXIV is atlas language only)
- A silicon mind, a Receiver organ, or non-local coupling
- An H1 or H2 proof, a 1M dual-gate, or hardware
- Affiliation with Fermi, any observatory, or any company named in the mythos

Brain-to-brain synchrony remains ordinary sensory-mediated neuroscience and is out of scope.
