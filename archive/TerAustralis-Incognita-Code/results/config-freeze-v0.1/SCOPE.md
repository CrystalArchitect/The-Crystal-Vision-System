# Scope of the H2 configuration freeze (V0.1)

## Interpretation Boundary

This record does **not** demonstrate:

- A 1M-token result, nor a dual-gate pass or fail
- Language-model quality
- Hardware bandwidth or dollar cost
- CrystalCore production quality, Optimus-relevance, or an xAI-relevant advantage
- Anything about H1
- Non-local, dimensional, or brain-to-brain information transfer

No new items were scored. This is a selection record over the validation
results. Final seed family sealed. Production `core/` unmodified.

## Freeze rule

Independently, per candidate, among the three validation configs:

1. highest agreement with dense reference
2. then highest quality-ratio LCB
3. then lowest analytical BW/ref
