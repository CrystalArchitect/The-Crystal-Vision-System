## The thesis
Star Trek was never only about warp drive. It was about a **shipyard that builds the future** and an **Academy that crews it**. Australia can offer both: launch geography, critical minerals, large-format metal additive manufacturing, flight-proven electric propulsion, and a defence-grade academy model (ADFA) adapted for deep-space crews.

## Six systems — fiction → Australian industrial reality
1. **Shipyard** — Titomic Kinetic Fusion (CSIRO-origin cold-spray AM). Boeing green-titanium tests and older Gilmour R&D intent exist; treat as **exploratory manufacturing**, not “we already print Starship hulls.” MMI grant history ≠ full production line.
2. **Impulse** — **Neumann Drive**: first commercial molybdenum solid-metal thruster fired in space (demonstrated on SpIRIT path). **Debris→propellant refuel** = CisLunar partnership *concept*, not flight-proven recycling. Pair with Gilmour hybrids for access; Hypersonix **SPARTAN** flew a Mach 5+ tech demo (Wallops, Feb 2026) — demonstrator, not ops product. ANSTO NTR = research only.
3. **Replicators** — Titomic / Amaero / CSIRO Lab22 metal AM for on-demand parts (ground now; orbital later).
4. **Sensors / SSA / optical links** — **Fleet Space** (Adelaide): commercial LEO + ExoSphere 3D subsurface mapping — **solid**. **EOS**: laser SSA/ranging proven; active debris “space control” / photon pressure — **in development**, don’t claim operational deorbit.
5. **Tricorder / sickbay** — AU medtech + quantum sensing heritage as *adjacency*, not productised Starfleet kit.
6. **Holodeck + Academy** — **Starfleet Academy Australia**: ADFA-style leadership + UNSW Canberra Space / RMIT + CSIRO science; classrooms at **Western Sydney Aerotropolis**, flight ops along **Qld launch corridor (Gilmour/Bowen)**. Holodeck v1 = high-fidelity MR/UE5 sims.
