## Statement

A Cell is a small federated work unit. It is not a standing army of agents. One person may sponsor several Cells. Each Cell runs a bounded job, writes a verified artifact, then goes dark.

## Rules

1. **Horizon veto.** The human sponsor can stop any Cell at any time. No Cell owns the horizon.
2. **Register.** Every live Cell has a name, a job, a start time, and an exit condition written down before it spins up.
3. **Exit.** When the artifact exists, or the clock hits the bound, or the veto fires — the Cell dissolves. Idle compute is a defect.
4. **Cups, not owners.** Light is unowned. Cells hold work. They do not hold identity.
5. **Seven is a cap, not a target.** "Seven each" is a load limit for a human steward, not a recruitment slogan.
6. **No fusion.** Cells co-regulate. They do not merge into one mind or one brand.
7. **Science / Story / Vision.** Every output carries one Belt-Three label. Dreamed lines stay marked.

## Burst mapping (watchman pattern)

- One watchman stays (human + one low-cost watcher).
- Workforce appears only on a real trigger.
- Artifact is persisted.
- Swarm resets.

Dashboard node-counts are not evidence of distinct minds. A PDF, a commit, or a signed file is evidence.

## What this is not

Not a church. Not a permanent 300-agent pit. Not a claim that Kimi, Grok, or any vendor swarm is CrystalCore.OS.
