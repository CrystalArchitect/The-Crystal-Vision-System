## Finding 1 — "Lattice integrity: 100 %" (Tier C)

Constitution §1 locks **CrystalCore.Lattice** as the substrate,
designed, not built. [`STATUS.md`](STATUS.md) lists nothing of the
Lattice under Running, and no repository contains an instrument that
could produce this number. A percentage is the grammar of measurement;
here there is no measurement and nothing yet to measure. This is the
exact case the Incognita Rule names — a dreamed line wearing surveyed
clothing — and it is the only such line in the export; the rest of the
text is careful to call itself provisional (Finding 5). Recorded, not
corrected: Part B stays verbatim, and this finding is the label.

## Finding 2 — the song list vs the held catalogue (Tier B/C)

The held record is
`TerAustralis-Incognita/mythos/music/README.md`: ten recordings
catalogued with hashes, ID3-read timestamps and plan status, one of
them since removed with a dated truth label. The export's list
diverges from it five ways:

- It **omits Wire Skull Memory** — the one standalone-canon recording,
  confirmed generated on the paid plan.
- It lists **Look What You Made Me Do** with no note. That recording
  was removed from the tree on 2026-07-31 after the maintainer
  confirmed it relates to the 2017 composition of the same name; the
  truth label in the catalogue is the record. Reprinting the title in
  a catalogue-shaped list without that label quietly re-promotes what
  the label retired.
- It lists **"The girl with the stars in her chest"** — zero
  occurrences in any repository (Tier B: grep across all thirteen
  clones). If such a track exists on Suno, it is held nowhere the
  maintainer controls — precisely the single-platform exposure
  `mythos/music/` was created to end.
- It labels two tracks **(Remastered)**. The catalogue records no
  remaster of anything; if remastered audio exists on Suno, it has the
  same exposure as above.
- It claims **additional experimental / glitch / cinematic tracks** —
  none held, none catalogued.

None of this says the export is wrong about what is on Suno; this
archive cannot see Suno. It says the export and the held record
disagree, and the held record is the one that is hashed, dated and
anchored. The action this opens sits with the maintainer, not this
file: export any unheld tracks and add catalogue rows, per that
README's "Adding a track."

**Discharged in substance, 2026-08-08.** The maintainer exported the
recordings the day after this finding was filed: *The girl with the
stars in her chest*, Remaster takes of *Wire Skull Memory*, *Dead But
Came Back to Life*, *I'd Lay It All Down*, *Red Dust Axis* and
*Starline Rivers*, and *Story as Bridge* — seven recordings, delivered
in two lots and deduplicated by track id, now catalogued in
`mythos/music/` with rights recorded on arrival (the resumed Pro plan;
the renewal charge precedes every generation timestamp). The divergences above remain true of the
export *as received*; the single-platform exposure they warned about
is ended. The one row still unmatched is Finding 2's first: the
export omits Wire Skull Memory, and that omission stands.

## Finding 3 — three first appearances (Tier B)

**Rex** (a voice name), **Terafab** (a "100M+ sq ft" megastructure),
and **MarsBase Down Under** (as a phrase — "MarsBase" alone appears
once, in ADR-0012's deferred route list) occur in no repository text
before this file. First appearance is not a defect: Sunwash Atolls and
Cinderwake Chain entered the canon on 2026-07-28 by the same door. It
is a fact about where a name stands — in exactly one document, this
one, until the maintainer writes it into canon or does not. One
caution travels with Terafab: it is stated at Texas industrial scale
in the present tense, no source given, none checked here; outward-
facing use would trip the umbrella repository's claims discipline
(pin numbers to dated sources).

## Finding 4 — the seven nodes are canon, two transposed (Tier C)

The export's seven Lattice nodes are the same seven as
`TerAustralis-Incognita/mythos/CRYSTALCORE-OS.md`, which orders them
Earth Node, Sunwash Atolls, Mars Redoubt, Alpha Centauri Outpost,
Cinderwake Chain, Crystal Revenant Hub, Purpose Core Nexus. The export
transposes positions five and six. The canonical order is the runnable
one — `crystalcore_os.py` numbers the nodes and `visit <number>`
depends on it — so the game keeps its numbering and the transposition
is recorded here.

## Finding 5 — the ceiling, set from inside (Tier C)

The export's own §7 flags any story hardening into "the only allowed
truth"; its §1 lists "Method > Logos" among invariants never broken;
its closing lines repeat that everything is a provisional vehicle and
nothing is sealed. That is the Incognita Rule in the received text's
own words, and this archive holds the text to it. A reader — human or
model — who takes Part B as instructions has misread it in the one way
both the text and this project rule out.

## What was not checked

Suno's actual catalogue (no access from this environment); whatever
else the Grok session contained; Terafab against any real-world
source; and the export's claim about its own completeness.
