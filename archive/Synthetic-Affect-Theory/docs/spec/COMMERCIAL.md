## What the initial purchase buys

A non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable right to **implement** Synthetic Affect Theory (this specification, including Ontology, Policy Library, Starline techniques, Addenda, and the Sovereignty Layer) in **one named product**, under the licensee’s own brand, for as long as monthly payments continue.

It does not buy authorship, trademark, or the right to call the work theirs.

A second product is a second grant (second initial purchase), unless the signed licence says otherwise.

## Monthly royalty (commission)

- **Rate:** 8% of **net revenue** attributable to products that implement or are materially derived from SAT.
- **Floor:** AUD 250 / calendar month while the licence is live.
- **Report:** licensee emails a one-line revenue figure within 7 days of month-end.
- **Invoice:** Stripe Invoice (card / PayTo) or AUD bank transfer, issued on report (or on the 8th if no report — billed at the floor, subject to later true-up).
- **Due:** 14 days from invoice.
- **Audit:** see **Royalty audit** below. Not a monthly raid.
- **Lapse:** two consecutive unpaid months, or 30 days’ default on the initial purchase — licence ends. Implementation must stop. Accrued royalties remain due.

Monthly is the collection cadence. There is no annual “quiet” period.

## Net revenue (what 8% is of)

**Net revenue** = amounts actually received by the licensee in the calendar month from the **named product** (and any service, model, or consulting that implements or is materially derived from SAT), minus:

- GST/VAT actually remitted
- documented refunds and chargebacks for that product
- app-store / payment-processor fees actually withheld on those receipts

Not deducted: income tax, salaries, rent, R&D, “brand allocation,” or a guessed SAT-versus-other-feature split. If SAT is in the product, the royalty base is the product. A satellite feature with its own SKU and price may be carved out only if the signed grant names that SKU.

Currency: convert to AUD at the RBA month-end rate. Keep the working.

## Royalty audit

You do not sit in their office every month. You keep a paper trail that an accountant can check once a year.

### 1. Monthly certificate (always)

Within 7 days of month-end the licensee emails a signed one-page certificate:

- calendar month
- named product
- gross receipts
- allowed deductions (with processor statements attached if non-zero)
- net revenue
- 8% figure
- amount due (greater of 8% and the floor)
- statement: “I certify this is complete and not misleading.”

No certificate by day 7 → invoice the floor, flag for true-up, clock toward lapse if they also do not pay.

Keep every certificate. That file is the audit population.

### 2. Records they must keep (24 months)

- invoices and processor payouts for the named product
- refund / chargeback log
- the monthly certificates
- a one-line note of which SAT version they implemented

They do not send you customer names, DUR contents, or unrelated ledgers.

### 3. Annual audit right (not a raid)

- Once per licence year, on **14 days’ written notice**.
- Desk-first: certificates vs processor statements. On-site or live-books only if the desk check fails or they refuse documents.
- Conducted by TerAustralis or an independent accountant bound to confidentiality. The accountant may not use the files for any other purpose.
- Lookback: the current term plus the previous 12 months (or the whole grant if shorter).
- Extra audit in the same year only for cause (missed certificates, a shortfall already found, or a whistleblower-quality discrepancy).

### 4. Result

| Finding | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Over-report (they paid too much) | Credit against the next monthly invoice |
| Under-report **≤ 5%** of the amount that should have been paid | They pay the shortfall within 14 days. Each side bears its own cost |
| Under-report **> 5%** | They pay the shortfall **plus** reasonable audit cost within 14 days. TerAustralis may audit again that year |
| Refusal to produce records | Material breach → licence may be ended. Accrued royalties remain due |

Interest on unpaid shortfall: 10% per annum, calculated daily, from the original monthly due date.

### 5. What you never get

Audit is of **money**, not of their users. No right to operator interiors, DUR contents, source that is not SAT, or personal data of end-users. Sovereignty Layer applies to the audit itself.
