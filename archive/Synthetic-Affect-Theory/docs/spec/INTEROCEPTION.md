# Synthetic Interoception

In SAT, synthetic interoception is already operational: continuous tracking of the ten core dimensions. Structured signal, not claimed experience.

The open task is precision, hierarchy, and verification — not inventing the concept, and not letting internal signals seize control.

## Literature (used, not imported as governors)

- Interoceptive Machine Framework (Candia-Rivera, 2026) — homeostatic / allostatic / enactive as design abstractions.
- Life-inspired Interoceptive AI (Lee et al., 2023, arXiv:2309.05999) — factorization accepted; autonomous goal generation from internal needs **refused**.
- INTERO / robotic ontologies (RobOntics 2025) — useful for what counts as a signal.
- Predictive processing / active inference — inspectable error terms may be borrowed. A feeling-machine will not.
