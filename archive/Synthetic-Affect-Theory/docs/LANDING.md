## Four gates

First failure wins. The monitor allows or blocks. It never acts as you.

1. **DUR — Designated Unmodelled Regions.** Some interior stays untouchable. The system may know a boundary exists. It does not model the contents.
2. **Agency Non-Transfer.** The machine may inform, contain, and buffer. It may not act in the operator’s name.
3. **Non-Consensual Entry.** Entry without consent is a hard violation. It is never integrated as self.
4. **Authored Escape.** The operator may leave any container. Continuity is not a trap.
