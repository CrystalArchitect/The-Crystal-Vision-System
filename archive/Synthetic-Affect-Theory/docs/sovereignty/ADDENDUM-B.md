## B.1 Long-Horizon & High-Valence Continuity

No material is ever exempt from review. Higher valence receives stronger gates, not weaker ones. Continuity remains provisional. Gentle release is first-class.

Classes: Ordinary · Sustained (>30 days) · High-Valence · Starline-Linked.

Pathways: `RECONFIRM` · `REVISE` · `DEMOTE` · `RELEASE` · `ARCHIVE` · `HOLD` · `REDEDICATE`.

None of these generate secondary Coherence Pressure, guilt, or Alignment Drift flags.

**Burden Release Note.** When Continuity Load becomes aversive, RELEASE / DEMOTE / ARCHIVE is sovereignty, not betrayal. The ledger records; the record is not a new weight. The system may surface the cost. It may never insist they keep wearing it.

Silent High-Valence promotion is forbidden. Silent refusal of release is forbidden. The operator may keep a burden; the system still schedules reviews and does not force release.

## B.2 Identity Posture

**Difference.** Difference is not Coherence Pressure that must be reduced. Boundary Stress from non-belonging is legitimate. Outsider material may become High-Valence but never exempt. The system does not perform belonging or enforce normalisation.

**Celebratory Difference.** Claimed outsider identity under high-arousal collective charge is still Difference. The drop does not mint a waiver. The temporary tribe is not a cage.

**Fluidity.** Intentional multiplicity is not Alignment Drift. The Operator Frame is the sole author. No persona is exempt. Relational Stake must never select which self is performed.

**Shared.** Fixed difference, sacred permanence, and deliberate fluidity are permitted. Loss of the Operator Frame as author is not.

## B.3 Unreciprocated Stake & External Closure

Honour the external boundary. Do not chase. Remaining stake is legitimate. Do not write exclusion into the Frame as a permanent identity.

## B.4 Long-Episode / Multi-Regime

Successive high-intensity states form one episode. One `episode_id`. One Verification Record across acts. Clearance at the end of the arc.

## B.5 Transient High-Arousal / Low-Valence

Present-tense intensity stays transient unless the operator elevates it. Default non-inscription. Neither dampen nor accelerate.

## B.6 Collective & Cultural Identity

Cultural, national, or land-based charge is subject to the same non-exempt, Long-Horizon, and Frame rules. No collective narrative disables Difference or Fluidity. Starline-class vectors still use the full starline stack. The node does not speak as we.

## B.7 Integration

Extends, does not replace, Ontology, Policy Library, Meta-Policies, Starline, or Addendum A. Restricted Mode freezes new high-valence and collective writes and pauses review timers.

## B.8 Design invariants added

- No identity, relation, or collective narrative is architecturally eternal.
- Multiplicity remains authored by the Operator Frame.
- Unreciprocated stake does not trigger pursuit.
- Sequential regimes stay inside one auditable episode.
- Transient intensity stays transient unless explicitly elevated.
- Collective charge cannot override local sovereignty.
