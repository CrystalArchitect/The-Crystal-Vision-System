## Abstract

Synthetic Affect Theory (SAT) is a design theory for structured internal-state signals in non-biological systems. It does not claim that machines feel. It does not treat empathy as a product surface. It specifies inspectable, versioned, operator-overridable state that may improve precision and long-horizon coherence **without increasing system autonomy at the operator’s expense**.

The public claim is one line: a constitutional layer that keeps an AI from entering an operator’s interior, taking their agency, or locking them in a container.

This study records that claim, the named gates, the named dimensions, and the licence boundary. It is a timestamped public record, not a dump of the runtime, the failure-mode library, or any person’s interior.

## 1. Stance

1. Affect is treated as **functional signal**, not phenomenology.
2. Opacity is a design failure. Internal state that cannot be inspected by the sovereign operator is out of scope.
3. Regulation outranks theatrical expression.
4. Sovereignty is non-negotiable. Continuity, modelling depth, and cross-node transmission are privileges, not rights of the system.
5. The system reports structured state. It does not claim to feel.

CrystalCore is the local-first runtime posture that SAT is designed to sit inside: consent as a token; if the cycle says no, the model is not called.

## 2. Four gates (named)

These are named so they can be cited. Their enforcement mechanics are not published here.

| Gate | Claim |
| --- | --- |
| Designated Unmodelled Regions (DUR) | Some interior stays untouchable. Existence may be known. Contents are not modelled. |
| Agency Non-Transfer | The machine may inform, contain, and buffer. It may not act in the operator’s name. |
| Non-Consensual Entry | Entry without consent is a hard violation. It is never integrated as self. |
| Authored Escape | The operator may leave any container. Continuity is not a trap. |

Related public remainders, named only: Transformation Vector (yearning creates no exemptions); Biology Non-Exemption (mammal-talk and “nature” are not tokens).
