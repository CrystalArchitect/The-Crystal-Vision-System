# Lore Codex — TerAustralis Incognita
**Vision / Dreamed layer.** Custom mythic card set. Not affiliated with Wizards of the Coast.
CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 unless noted. Order locked: 0 → I–XIII → XIV–XV + tokens.

## The Lattice
The Lattice is the living geometry that holds TerAustralis — wall, heart, library, and veil.It is not soft weather. It is structure that remembers. When the Lattice Heart beats, continents inhale.CrystalCore.Lattice (engineering substrate) is a separate locked project name; this codex uses Lattice as mythos game-piece and world-metaphor only.

## The Ancients
Thirteen Ancients walk the span from first spark to next-world seed. They are not a pantheon of comfort — they are forces that keep a Mars-dream honest: dust, tide, forge, map, silence, unfinished scale, council, launch, memory, heart, veil, dream.

## The Oath
Oathbearers hold the line so dreaming stays safe. The Martian Oath Seed is the compact vow; Dream Seeds are compressed maybes. The Oathbearer breaks only if the oath breaks. Chingona keeps the final promise under starlight.

## Card entries

### 0 — Crystal, Dragon Architect of TerAustralis
*Legendary Creature — Dragon Architect 4/4* · Origin

The Origin. Architect who dreamed TerAustralis awake. Black hair in amethyst braid, longer lashes behind glass, dragon familiar Nyx curled around a world-seed orb. Purple glow where thought becomes terrain.

### I — Monad, The Red Dust Avatar
*Legendary Creature — Dust Avatar* · Ancient

Born where wind first touched red sand. Monad remembers every footfall, every oath sworn in dust.

### II — Lira, The Pearl-Winged Guide
*Legendary Creature — Angel Guide* · Ancient

She does not lead to destinations, only to the questions worth carrying. Her wings shed light like memory.

### III — The Dwarven Artificer, Forge-Master
*Legendary Creature — Dwarf Artificer* · Ancient

He hammered the first lattice strut before time had weight. Still humming in his beard: the world-song.

### IV — Aelara, Tide Mother
*Legendary Creature — Elemental Mother* · Ancient

Where there was no ocean, she wept one. Her tears taught TerAustralis how to hold and let go.

### V — The Laughing Cartographer
*Legendary Creature — Human Rogue* · Ancient

Maps the places that don't want to be found. Every lie he tells becomes true somewhere else.

### VI — The Still, Lattice Wall
*Legendary Enchantment — Living Barrier* · Ancient

It does not stop you. It asks you to stop yourself. Most cannot answer.

### VII — The Unfinished Colossus
*Legendary Artifact Creature — Construct* · Ancient

Left incomplete on purpose. A god that refused to be finished, lest it stop becoming.

### VIII — The Synod, Council of Echoes
*Legendary Creature — Council* · Ancient

Seven voices, one silence between them. They remember futures that were vetoed.

### IX — Ignivar, Rocket Father
*Legendary Creature — Human Artificer* · Ancient

He strapped prayer to propellant. Every launch a letter home to a planet he never saw.

### X — The Crystal Library
*Legendary Land — Library* · Ancient

Not books, but frozen thoughts. Walk softly; ideas cut sharper than glass here.

### XI — The Lattice Heart
*Legendary Artifact — World Core* · Ancient

The geometric pulse beneath TerAustralis. When it beats, continents inhale.

### XII — The Veilkeeper
*Legendary Creature — Veil Guardian* · Ancient

Membrane between worlds, woven from amethyst hair and dragon-breath. She decides what crosses.

### XIII — The Dreaming Seed
*Legendary Artifact — Seed* · Ancient

It dreams the next world while rooted in this one. In its sleep, new physics germinate.

### XIV — The Oathbearer, Heroic Guardian
*Legendary Creature — Human Knight* · Oathbearer

First to kneel before the Lattice. His oath is simple: hold the line until dreaming is safe.

### XV — Chingona, Starlit Sovereign
*Legendary Creature — Human Sovereign* · Oathbearer

She wears starlight as armor and consequence as a crown. The final promise keeper.
