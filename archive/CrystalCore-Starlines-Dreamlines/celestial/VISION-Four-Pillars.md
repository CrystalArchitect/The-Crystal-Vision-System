## What is checked, and what is offered

Written out this way because the frame mixes both, and a reader a year from now
will not remember which was which.

### Checked

**Atlas — Mercator, and a correction.** The word entered cartography with
Mercator's map-book. But he did **not** name it for the Titan holding up the
sky: his own preface names Atlas, King of Mauretania, a legendary philosopher
and astronomer credited with the first celestial globe. Later editions changed
the frontispiece to the Titan and the Titan became the standard gloss. See
*The word atlas, 1595* in `PRECEDENTS.md`. Publication began 1585 and finished
posthumously in 1595; Ortelius's *Theatrum Orbis Terrarum* (1570) is generally
called the first modern atlas.

**Time — the gap it names is real, and is now partly closed.** A reading is how
somebody hears a work on a day, and until 11 August 2026 the shape could not
hold that: revising a reading destroyed the hearing it replaced. `revise()` and
`history()` now keep the chain, and `asOf` dates a hearing. That much was
buildable and is built. Walking placements *as a sequence in a drawing* is not.

### Offered

**Aeris — mind, atmosphere, dreams.** Latin *aer / aeris*, "of the air". Note
the form is ambiguous: `aeris` is equally the genitive of *aes*, bronze or
money. The further step — dreams and inner states as aerial or pneumatic
phenomena — is the holder's, and no source is recorded here for a specific
classical link between *aeris* and dreaming. The nearest real tradition,
Aristotle's *On Dreams* and the medieval humoral accounts, has vapours rising
from the stomach rather than from the air, and *pneuma* is breath rather than
*aer*.

In the code as it stands, **AERIS is an edition name** — `CrystalCore.OS AERIS
/ VAULT 12`, heading the Starline Consent Transport Protocol — and the Vercel
project deploying this repository. It is not a dream layer. If the frame is to
match the code, one of the two has to move.

**Britannica — a reference work, named as a standard.** It occurs nowhere in
any repository in this project. Nothing resolves against it, imports it, or
depends on it, and naming it here is not an institutional affiliation. Worth
holding in view: *sovereignty is the default, and silent dependency and soft
lock-in are architectural failures.* An external work named as the layer that
"stabilises" the others is a definitional dependency, and this project already
has a defining source text in its Constitution and ADRs.

## Terminology

The source text for this frame used the reserved word in its Time bullet. It is
**starlines** here. `Indigenous-Data-Sovereignty.md` holds that the reserved
word is never a component name — it belongs to the First Peoples of this land,
not to a piece of software — and Starline (the map) and Dreamline (the
traveller) are this project's own coinages. A test in this directory enforces
it; the substitution is recorded rather than made silently.
