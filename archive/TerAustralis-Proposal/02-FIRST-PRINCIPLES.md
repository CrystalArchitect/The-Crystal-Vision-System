# 02 — First Principles

**TerAustralis Incognita**

These are the non-negotiable constraints derived from the problems. They are not preferences. They are design requirements.

---

## 1. Continuity of consciousness is sacred

The primary concern is the safe carriage of living minds across time, distance, and technological change.  
No architecture may treat continuity as disposable.

## 2. Multiplanetary expansion is required for civilisational durability

A single-planet species remains a single point of failure.  
Extending the light of consciousness across more than one world is the next necessary enlargement of the human project.

## 3. Distance is the quarantine, not an error

High latency and intermittent connectivity are normal operating conditions for multiplanetary and deep-isolation systems.  
Architectures that assume continuous high-bandwidth links are mismatched to the domain.

## 4. Fail-safe must be local isolation

When external links degrade or are compromised, systems must default to local, coherent isolation rather than fail open into external optimisation or control.

## 5. Sovereignty is the default state

People, communities, and nations retain ownership of memory, decisions, and critical infrastructure.  
Silent dependency and soft lock-in are architectural failures.

## 6. Consent must be active and inspectable

No significant action or modification of continuity-critical state without clear, revocable, inspectable consent.  
Consent is a runtime property, not a policy document.

## 7. Vision and infrastructure are inseparable

Dreams remain inert until given physical form.  
Infrastructure without orienting vision remains logistics.  
Both are required.

## 8. Geographic and industrial redundancy is mandatory

Concentration of recovery, feedstock, energy, and launch capacity in limited geographies increases systemic risk.  
Southern Hemisphere capacity is a necessary counterweight.

---

These principles govern every subsequent document in this repository.
