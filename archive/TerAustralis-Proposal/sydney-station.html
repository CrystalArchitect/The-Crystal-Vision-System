Sydney Station.

An xAI data centre, Optimus-grade robotics, and a Neuralink-grade BCI clinic — on Australian ground. Two campuses, one rule. Power first. Consent first. Red dust on the boots.

This is not an official xAI, Tesla, or Neuralink project. It is a structured invitation from TerAustralis Incognita. Precincts named below already exist. The stack, the megawatts, and the clinic protocol are proposed. No site is selected. No agreement is claimed. The X original: Sydney Station, 13 Aug 2026. Sibling brief: tiger-ruby-cosmic-cliff.

Heavy plant in the west. Cortex at Westmead. Not a map with pins — precincts, named in prose. The fibre between them is proposed. The hospital and the employment lands are not.

Six ground rules

Learn from Memphis. Obey NSW. Protect the nervous system. Each rule is a fail-closed gate, same architecture as the software: refusal is the default.

01 · Bring your own power

Large loads underwrite new generation and pay for the network. No household subsidy.

02 · Do not drink the aquifer

Cooling from treated / recycled water. Drinking water stays for people.

03 · Permit the plant

No unpermitted turbines, no unpermitted gas. The plant is legal or it does not run.

04 · Two campuses, one command

West builds the body. Westmead holds the clinic and the ethics. One invitation, two addresses.

05 · The channel is not a merge

Medical cursor bandwidth. Kill switch. Family and security review. Explant plan published before write.

06 · Red dust landing

Evidence before assumption. Pausable. Publishable. If the narrative is wrong, the precincts are still there.
