# 01 — The Problems

**TerAustralis Incognita**

These are the actual problems the work exists to solve. Everything else follows from them.

---

## 1. Single-Planet Concentration Risk

A single-planet species is a single point of failure for everything consciousness has become.  
All current human continuity — biological, cultural, technological, and civilisational — remains concentrated on one world.  
Any sufficiently severe disruption (natural or otherwise) ends the experiment.

**Problem:** There is no durable second locus of human continuity.

---

## 2. Hemispheric and Geographic Concentration

Even on Earth, heavy-lift, recovery, and industrial capacity for multiplanetary operations remains heavily concentrated in a limited set of geographies.  
Southern Hemisphere physical and sovereign capacity is underdeveloped relative to the scale of the ambition.

**Problem:** Lack of geographic redundancy increases systemic fragility.

---

## 3. High-Latency Intelligence Gap

Multiplanetary and deep-isolation operations will run under high latency, intermittent connectivity, and contested bandwidth.  
Most current intelligence architectures assume relatively continuous, high-bandwidth links and fail open or degrade poorly when those links disappear.

Local-first inference and memory are no longer the gap: mature open-source components now run models on commodity hardware without external dependency, and flight software has operated under real light-time delay for decades.

**Problem:** What does not exist is the layer above that substrate — one that makes consent enforceable at runtime rather than a deployment posture, continuity of memory and agency guaranteed rather than incidental, and fail-safe isolation a specified, tested behaviour rather than a side effect of running locally.

---

## 4. Continuity of Consciousness Treated as Optional

Many systems optimise for throughput, engagement, or capability while treating memory, agency, and continuity of the human operator as secondary.  
Under distance and isolation this becomes a civilisational risk, not a product preference.

**Problem:** Continuity of consciousness is not yet treated as a hard architectural constraint.

---

## 5. Dreams Without Infrastructure

The long human dream of the stars remains largely aspirational while the physical substrate (recovery geography, feedstock, energy, high-latency systems, and continuity protocols) is incomplete.

**Problem:** Vision without infrastructure remains fantasy. Infrastructure without vision remains mere logistics. The gap between the two is still wide.

---

## 6. Sovereignty and Consent Under Distance

As systems become more capable and more remote, the default risk is soft lock-in, silent dependency, and loss of meaningful human control.  
Distance amplifies this risk.

**Problem:** Sovereignty and active consent are not yet default properties of the architectures being built for multiplanetary conditions.

---

These six problems define the scope.  
The rest of the repository is the response.
