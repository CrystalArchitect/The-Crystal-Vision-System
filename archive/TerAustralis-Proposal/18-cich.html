CICH

Cognitive Immune Complex. Working model. Not a public theory. Not a product. Not a homepage item.

Working model · not doctrine

0. Status

CICH is a personal epistemic mapping system. It is not a doctrine, not an academic school, and not software. It is a diagnostic instrument for scanning, mapping, and auditing the structures that shape reasoning, identity, and discourse.

The moment it hardens into unfalsifiable identity armor, the instrument has failed its own gate. This brief records the open map. It does not ask anyone to believe it. It is not a door on www.teraustralis.com.au.

1. Definition

The model treats cognition the way an immune system treats a body: complexes, boundaries, responses, and pathogens (rigid ideas, tribal hardening, unfalsifiable claims, identity capture). It is used to watch reasoning patterns, not to win the local exchange.

4. Operating principles (inside the model)

  • Open-map over closed map.
  • No masters. No external authority, ideology, or persona overrides the falsification gates.
  • Identity is flex/armor, not essence. Personas are temporary tools, not permanent residence.
  • Direction: chaotic cognitive material → clearer structure through mapping. Not pure destruction. Not pure preservation.
