## 0. Status

CICH is a personal epistemic mapping system. It is not a doctrine, not an academic school, and not software. It is a diagnostic instrument for scanning, mapping, and auditing the structures that shape reasoning, identity, and discourse.

The moment it hardens into unfalsifiable identity armor, the instrument has failed its own gate. This brief records the open map. It does not ask anyone to believe it.

This document lives on the proposal site. It is not a door on www.teraustralis.com.au.

## 1. Definition

The model treats cognition the way an immune system treats a body: complexes, boundaries, responses, and pathogens (rigid ideas, tribal hardening, unfalsifiable claims, identity capture).

It is used to watch reasoning patterns, not to win the local exchange.

## 2. Components

| Part | Job |
|---|---|
| Lattice | Interconnected beliefs, assumptions, values, identity claims. Open = permeable, revisable, oriented toward falsification. Hostile = sealed, tribal, self-reinforcing, low falsification readiness. Most discourse failures mistake a Hostile Lattice for neutral ground. |
| Gates | Explicit falsification points. A claim must meet evidence, counter-example, predictive failure, or internal contradiction. Not optional decoration. |
| Armor | Identity layer around the self-narrative. Healthy = flex (adaptive, temporary embodiment without capture). Pathological = rigid hardening (identity as fortress). |
| CICH Lens | Open-map observation of reasoning patterns, boundaries, and engagement protocols. Structure over the local win. |
| ESF / ESP | Epistemic Sentinel Function. Continuous audit for overconfidence, tribal capture, unfalsifiable claims, master-seeking, certainty addiction, lattice closure. |

## 4. Operating principles (inside the model)

- Open-map over closed map.
- No masters. No external authority, ideology, or persona overrides the falsification gates.
- Identity is flex/armor, not essence. Personas are temporary tools for testing boundaries, not permanent residence.
- Direction: chaotic cognitive material → clearer structure through mapping. Not pure destruction. Not pure preservation.
