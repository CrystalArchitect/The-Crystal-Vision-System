Vision

This page is aspiration and philosophy — a direction of travel, not a description of current capability. For what exists and is tested today, see the Verification page.

TerAustralis Incognita — “the known that charts the future”

A long-horizon vision for sovereign, consent-bound intelligence.

CrystalCore is the working foundation. TerAustralis Incognita is the larger story it's meant to serve: using deep, known wisdom — relational protocols, custodianship, consent — to guide how intelligence might responsibly extend outward over time. What follows is intentionally framed as where this could go, not what it does now.

The idea

Wisdom as the thing that charts the way.

The premise is a reversal of the usual order. Rather than building capability first and asking about ethics afterward, the vision puts the known first — long-held relational principles, consent, custodianship, the responsibility one generation holds for the next — and treats those as the things that should steer what gets built.

It draws, with respect, on Songlines as a way of thinking about navigation: that moving through a territory carries obligations — permission before passage, right relationship maintained, knowledge held in trust across generations. The aspiration is to express those obligations as properties a system actually honours, rather than values left in a document.

Australia's abundant solar resources and a sovereign, local-first approach to intelligence form the imagined material base. The far-horizon image — solar-powered systems extending such protocols outward, even toward a multiplanetary future — is deliberately distant. It is the direction the philosophy points, offered as vision.

Permission before passage

Nothing acts without appropriate consent. In the current framework this already appears as consent flags and fail-closed reasoning; the vision extends the same principle outward.

Right relationship provenance

Every conclusion traceable to its sources. The framework implements this today as provenance metadata; the vision frames it as a relational, not merely technical, commitment.

Custodianship across generations

What matters is preserved and protected over time — expressed today as family-priority preservation under memory pressure, imagined more broadly as generational stewardship.

Cyclical balance reflection

Pauses for coherence and reflection as a kind of discipline. A design value the framework gestures at, offered in the vision as an ongoing practice rather than a feature.

From built to envisioned

A phased direction — honestly marked.

The first step is done and verified. Everything past it is roadmap: appropriate for higher-criticality systems, and not yet implemented.

01 Property-based tests on safety invariants. Veto, consent, coherence, pruning, durability — checked across randomized inputs. Built & passing
02 Model checking (TLA+ / Apalache). Exhaustive or symbolic verification of a hand-written model of the agent orchestration and veto logic. Future — not started
03 Mission-time LTL runtime monitors. Bounded-LTL monitors that watch execution traces on constrained hardware and trigger safe modes on violation. Future — not started
04 Layered assurance for higher-criticality deployment. The combination of the above, applied to whatever a future system actually requires. Future — not started

line of demarcation — built today: a four-layer Python framework with 118 passing tests, two end-to-end demos, running offline on commodity hardware (see Verification and Framework). Steps 02–04 above, and every cosmic or multiplanetary image on this page, are direction and philosophy — not current capability. The separation is deliberate.
