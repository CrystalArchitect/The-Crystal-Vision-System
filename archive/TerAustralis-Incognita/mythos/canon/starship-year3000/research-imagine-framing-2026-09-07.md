## D) Proposed dense bullets

### Present column (10 bullets — fact-checked spine)
1. **Treaty door open, not a free pass:** US–AU Technology Safeguards Agreement in force **23 Jul 2024** — US vehicles *can apply* to operate from AU territory/waters; every activity still needs AU licences/permits; ITAR still binds.
2. **Policy fit published:** Commonwealth **Statement on Space (21 Jul 2026)** names five focus areas — trusted services · spaceflight ecosystem · platforms · microgravity · exploration — matching access / stay / build / return verbs without claiming delivery.
3. **Sector base (cite carefully):** ASA materials cite space SMEs at **A$4.6bn turnover (2021 baseline in Statement)**; Jul 2025 fact sheet also cited ~17k FTE / ~620 orgs in pack — prefer dated labels on the page.
4. **Ship 40 adjacency (Jul–Aug 2026):** First intact Starship upper-stage ocean salvage guided to waters off **Christmas Island**; ASA/Gov coordination; tow ≠ pad MoU. Proves southern IO logistics matter under the TSA era.
5. **Bowen / Gilmour (Qld):** Licensed orbital spaceport; mid-inclination / rotation-assist geography; Eris **TF1 30 Jul 2025** anomaly (short burn); **TF2 ~early 2027*** — not orbital yet; ElaraSat on SpaceX rideshare ≠ Starship partnership.
6. **Southern Launch (SA):** **Koonibba** commercial re-entry ops (from Feb 2025; multiple returns through 2026*); **Whalers Way** polar/SSO orbital complex advancing under approvals; Series A **A$25m** (incl. **A$10m NRF**) Jun 2026.
7. **Neumann Stay verb:** Commercial solid-metal EP on SpaceX Transporters (**ND-25** T-16/T-17 cadence 2026; **ND-50** CarbSAR Jan 2026). Debris→fuel / CisLunar = **roadmap only**.
8. **Build verb (pilots):** Titomic cold-spray + CSIRO **Lab22** metal AM — real aerospace/defence traction (incl. NASA SAA path in pack); **not** a Starship hull factory.
9. **Crew verb (model, not charter):** Academy framing = Western Sydney Aerotropolis sims + ADFA-style leadership + Qld flight corridor — proposal spine, **no Academy MoU claimed**.
10. **Hard blockers stay visible:** No Starship MoU · ELA not live · methalox industrial chain for Starship-class cadence **not public** · SpIRIT ≠ Gilmour · concept art ≠ certification.
