## Design Principle: Warrant-Labelling in Action

The Pathway Log demonstrates how the **Sovereign Lattice covenant** (never hide sources, never build on false knowledge) works in practice. Every claim in every entry is labeled by its epistemic source:

- **[Fact]** — Observable, verified, recorded during the engagement
- **[Inference]** — Our interpretation of what was said or observed
- **[Assumption]** — What we're assuming to be true without explicit confirmation
- **[Vision]** — What we hope or believe should be true (aspirational)

This labeling is not decoration. It is the commitment: we will show our work. Observers can audit which beliefs are grounded in field observation and which are hope or assumption.

## Entry Template

Each Pathway Log entry follows this structure:

### **Entry N: [Engagement Name/Date]**

#### Engagement Context
**Who:** [Person/organization name, role, context]  
**When:** [Date/timeframe]  
**Where:** [Location/medium]  
**Format:** [Conversation, presentation, workshop, interview, etc.]  
**Initiator:** [Who reached out to whom? Who set the agenda?]

**[Fact]** Observable engagement details (what happened, who was present, what was discussed).

---

#### Intent: What Were We Testing?
State the question or hypothesis that drove this engagement:
- **Roadmap question:** [What did we need to learn for the roadmap?]
- **Hypothesis:** [What did we expect to hear or find?]
- **Success metric:** [How would we know if the engagement was useful?]

---

#### Warrant-Labelled Engagement Notes

**What they said (direct quotes or close paraphrase):**
- "[Quote]" — [Name, timestamp/session indicator]
- "[Quote]" — [Name, timestamp/session indicator]

**[Inference]** What we understood them to mean:
- [Our interpretation of the significance of their statement]
- [What this suggests about their position, capability, or need]

**[Assumption]** What we're assuming without explicit confirmation:
- [Gaps in what was said that we're filling in]
- [Beliefs about their motivations or constraints that weren't stated directly]

**[Vision]** What we hope is true based on this engagement:
- [Aspirational interpretation of what's possible]
- [What we believe *should* follow from what we learned]

---

#### Learnings: How Did This Engagement Change Our Understanding?

**[Fact]** New facts we can now claim:
- [Specific, verifiable information we extracted]
- [Capability or willingness they demonstrated]
- [Concrete constraint or opportunity we identified]

**[Inference]** New interpretations:
- [What pattern does this engagement fit into?]
- [How does this compare with other engagements?]
- [What does this tell us about the landscape?]

**[Assumption]** Assumptions we can now test:
- [Beliefs that need follow-up confirmation]
- [Areas of uncertainty revealed by this engagement]

**[Vision]** Confirmations or updates to our vision:
- [Does the engagement support or challenge our roadmap intent?]
- [Are the pathways we're building feasible? Valuable?]

---

#### Feedback Loop: How Does This Feed Back Into the Roadmap?

**Question answered:**
- [Which roadmap question did this engagement help answer?]
- [Evidence:** [Fact/Inference/Assumption/Vision label from above that addresses it]]

**New question raised:**
- [What follow-up question does this engagement create?]
- [Who should we engage with next?]
- [What will change in our approach?]

**Roadmap adjustment:**
- [What in the 90-day roadmap changes based on this learning?]
- [Priority shift? Timeline? New blocker? New opportunity?]
- [Who needs to know this learning? How will it propagate?]

---

#### Reflection: What Will We Do Differently?

**Engagement quality:**
- What worked in this engagement?
- What would we do differently next time?
- What's a blind spot we discovered?

**System response:**
- How quickly did we recognize the learning?
- Who did we communicate it to?
- What was the decision lag between observation and adjustment?

## Authority & Governance

**What warrants an entry:** Any engagement where we're testing a roadmap question or extracting a learning about the landscape (Australian industrial sovereignty, Stellar Lattice architecture, governance, technical feasibility, stakeholder readiness).

**Who documents entries:** Claude Code (autonomous) or Crystal directly (if providing explicit feedback). Each entry notes its author.

**What "published" means:** Entries are posted to a public Slack channel or public GitHub location where external observers can read, audit, and challenge the warrant-labelling.

**Dispute resolution:** If a reader challenges the labeling (e.g., "you said [Fact] but that's really [Inference]"), the entry is updated with an amendment. The amendment is dated and attributed.
