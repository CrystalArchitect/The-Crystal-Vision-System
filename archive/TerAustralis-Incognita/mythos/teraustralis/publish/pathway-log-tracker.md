[`memory/projects/90-Day-Roadmap/CURRENT.md`](../../../memory/projects/90-Day-Roadmap/CURRENT.md)

**Column notes:**

- **Who** — real person/organization name and role, not a category
  ("industry contact").
- **Ask** — the specific question or request made of them, one line.
- **Learned** — one-line summary of the outcome; the full nuance
  (quotes, inference, assumptions, vision) belongs in the linked entry,
  not this row.
- **Warrant tier** — the strongest epistemic label the "Learned"
  summary earns: `[Fact]` (they confirmed it directly), `[Inference]`
  (our read of what they meant), `[Assumption]` (we're filling a gap),
  or `[Vision]` (aspirational, not confirmed). Never round up.
- **Full entry** — relative link to the dated entry file (e.g.
  `pathway-log-entry-1-2026-10-15.md`), written against
  `pathway-log-framework.md`'s template.
