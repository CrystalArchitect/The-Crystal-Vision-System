### 2026-09-04 — Caught and corrected a supply-chain-verification claim before it went further

**Status: Gap found & partially closed.**

Roadmap item #7 (Small Council) sent outreach on 2026-09-03 to four
organizations (Standards Australia, Lynas Rare Earths, Magellan
Aerospace, Equatorial Launch Australia) proposing a formal supply-chain
verification process built around an "ASA Statement of Engagement."

On 2026-09-04, re-verification against the actual Gmail account and the
public record found:

- **One factual error, now corrected.** The Magellan outreach described
  it as having Australian manufacturing operations and positioned it as
  a "Tier 3 manufacturing anchor." Magellan Aerospace's real facilities
  are in Canada, the US, the UK, India, and Poland — not Australia. A
  correction and withdrawal was sent to Magellan the same day.
- **One structural problem, not yet solved.** Standards Australia (the
  real body the outreach meant, distinct from both the "Australian
  Standards Association" a prior session's PR fabricated outright, and
  from the Australian Space Agency, which the subject line accidentally
  also evoked) replied that certification, verification, auditing, or
  endorsement of supply chains is not within their remit at all. The
  entire "ASA Statement of Engagement" mechanism this roadmap item was
  built around does not exist as conceived. No replacement mechanism has
  been identified yet.
- **Phase 2 outreach paused** pending that redesign, per the operator's
  direct instruction, rather than proceeding on the original Oct 7-11
  schedule with an unresolved mechanism underneath it.

**Why this is the entry, not a quieter fix.** This project's own
governance record (`memory/projects/90-Day-Roadmap/CURRENT.md`, "PR #146
reconciliation") already documents one prior incident of unsourced,
fabricated positioning claims about these same kinds of real companies.
Catching a live recurrence of that pattern, verifying it against the
real world instead of assuming either way, and correcting what was
already sent is exactly the "execution vs. ambition gap" this Ledger
exists to record — a closed gap is still a gap.

**Full detail:** [`specs/pathway-log-entry-week-7-9.md`](../../../specs/pathway-log-entry-week-7-9.md)
"Corrections found on re-verification" section.
