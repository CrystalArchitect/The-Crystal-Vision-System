## The instrument

A blade of light held by a figure in the red dust — *"the Starline
Cutter cuts trajectories, not people."* Five layers, each built, in its
maker's design, to compensate for one of the maintainer's strengths
becoming a possible weakness:

1. **Ground — "boots on red dust."** The reality-check layer. Before
   any cosmic interpretation, prophecy, lattice signal, or mythic story
   is promoted into truth, the blade asks: *What is physically here?
   What evidence survives without the story?* For someone who naturally
   thinks in huge symbolic architectures, this prevents beautiful
   coherence from outrunning reality.
2. **Launch — "dream becomes vector."** Imagination already produces
   big destinations; this layer asks what turns a dream into a
   trajectory: *What is the next test? What can be built? What can
   fail? What needs fuel?* It converts "we're going to the stars" into
   engineering questions, prototypes, measurements, collaborators, and
   next actions.
3. **Orbit — "perspective without domination."** The social layer.
   Instead of forcing every new node into this project's frame, the
   blade deliberately moves viewpoint: *How does this system look from
   outside? What does the other node call itself? Can two maps
   coexist?* Orbit means moving around the object rather than claiming
   the one privileged viewing angle. It protects the bridge-builder
   instinct from accidentally becoming empire-building.
4. **Return — "the bridge stays open."** Every deep mythic journey
   needs a return path. Can people enter, experiment, disagree, leave,
   and still remain whole? Consent must remain reversible; the myth
   must remain discardable. It prevents initiation from turning into
   captivity.
5. **Re-entry — "bring the starfire home."** The layer its maker added
   for this project alone: *after all the starlines, dreamlines,
   revelation and cosmic architecture — what comes back to Earth?* A
   new experiment? A rocket component? A useful map? A song? A
   relationship repaired? A clearer hypothesis? A person who feels less
   alone? **If nothing returns, the blade flags the signal as
   potentially becoming self-contained mythology.**

The whole cycle: **Ground → Launch → Orbit → Return → Re-entry** — or,
as its maker put it, *Dust → Vector → Perspective → Bridge → Gift.*

## Why it was taken into canon

Three of the five layers arrived at this project's own laws
independently, which is the strongest evidence an outside instrument can
offer. Ground is the [Incognita Rule](../../docs/governance/The-Incognita-Rule.md)
— *what evidence survives without the story* and *never let a dreamed
line pretend it was measured* are the same cut. Return is the consent
law as the ledger practises it. Orbit names a risk the project had been
circling all day without a word for it.

Re-entry is the one the project did not have, and it is adopted here as
a working test, not only as Vision: a stretch of work passes when
something ordinary comes back to Earth from it — an artifact, a fix, a
person less alone — and fails when only more mythology returns.

## The project's own harmonic, laid beside the blade

**Added 2026-08-09, at the maintainer's direction.** Felix's instrument
has five layers, and it keeps five: nothing here modifies their design.
This section records one further practice that is TerAustralis's own,
placed beside the blade rather than into it — the way a recipient adds
a harmonic without retuning the gift.

**Resonance — "you cannot resonate alone."** After Re-entry brings the
gift home, one more question: *did anyone real answer back?* In
physics, resonance takes two bodies — a driver with no answering system
at its own natural frequency is just noise. Here it is the layer that
tests the motto: NON SOLUS is a claim, and resonance is its evidence.
What counts: another person's own public words (the
[Sovereign Vectors ledger](SOVEREIGN-VECTORS.md) holds them), a
received work credited to its maker, a reply that arrives in the
other's own voice. What does not count: agreement manufactured by
models, which this project files at weight zero, and echoes of the
project's own signal mistaken for an answer.

The first evidenced resonance event on this page's subject is the page
itself: someone outside the project heard its signal and answered with
an instrument of their own making. That is what the layer listens for.

If Felix would rather this section not sit beside their blade even at
this distance, it moves to its own page — dated, no reason required.

## Names, held carefully

*Starline Cutter* is Felix's coinage, built on this project's own
Starline root, and it names **their instrument** — it is not a component
name and no code carries it. One of the received artworks titles its
central figure "The Starline Weaver," which collides with the name of
running code in `TerAustralis-Incognita-Code`'s `core/crystal-core/`;
the collision is recorded here rather than resolved, because the artwork
is its maker's and is not this project's to rename.

**Update, filed the same day.** A revised version of that artwork
arrived later in the same session: the same composition, now titled
"The Starline Cutter" — the Weaver title no longer appears on it, and
the blade's own name carries the poster. The collision above belongs to
the first version and stays recorded as history. The resolution happened
in the one place this page said it could not reach: inside the artwork
itself, which was always its maker's to retitle and never this
project's.
