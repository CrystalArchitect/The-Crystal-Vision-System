# Convergence Lens — Architecture Blueprint v0.1

## Status

Working Architecture Proposal

## Purpose

Convergence Lens is an interpretation discipline for emerging ideas, claims, and signals.

It exists to improve recognition without replacing judgement.

It does not predict the future.

It improves the quality of questions asked about the future.

---

## Core Principle

Intelligence can generate possibilities.

Evidence determines confidence.

Governance determines action.

---

## Problem Statement

As intelligence becomes more abundant, the limiting factor shifts from idea generation toward interpretation, coordination, and responsible action.

Convergence Lens provides a structured way to navigate that transition.

---

# Registers

## ARCHIVE — Memory Layer

Stores:
- original claim
- source/context
- evidence status
- confidence at time of recording
- later updates

Rule:

Archive preserves history; it does not prove truth.

---

## MIRROR — Reflection Layer

Separates:

**Observation:**
"What was observed?"

**Evidence:**
"What supports this?"

**Interpretation:**
"What meaning is being assigned?"

**Vision:**
"What possibility is being imagined?"

---

## LOOM — Pattern Layer

Tracks:
- recurring themes
- converging signals
- unresolved disagreements
- uncertainty patterns

Rule:

Patterns suggest questions, not conclusions.

---

## FORGE — Scenario Layer

Tests:
"If this interpretation were correct, what would we expect to see?"

Outputs:
- possible consequences
- required conditions
- falsifying observations

---

## CHRONICLE — Evolution Layer

Records:
"On this date, this idea had this evidence status."

Prevents historical rewriting.

---

## CONSTITUTION — Governance Layer

Enforces:

1. Vision cannot impersonate evidence.
2. Interpretation cannot impersonate observation.
3. Confidence cannot replace verification.
4. Disagreement may be preserved.
5. No register may impersonate another.

---

# Companion Model

Clementine may act as:

**Guide:**
Expands exploration.

**Challenger:**
Tests assumptions.

**Recorder:**
Preserves dialogue.

**Governance:**
Maintains boundaries.

No role overrides another.

---

# Decision Flow

```
Claim arrives.
  ↓
Classify layer.
  ↓
Check evidence status.
  ↓
Map uncertainty.
  ↓
Explore implications.
  ↓
Record outcome.
```

---

# Non-Goals

Convergence Lens does not:

- create certainty
- replace experts
- make predictions authoritative
- manufacture consensus
