# Crystal Vision

**Status:** Foundational definition  
**Date:** 2026-07-29

---

## Definition

**Crystal Vision** is the overarching living framework from which TerAustralis Incognita and CrystalCore OS emerge.

It is not a single project or product. It is a coherent set of principles and long-horizon intentions that guide the work:

1. **Continuity of consciousness is sacred**  
   The primary concern is the safe carriage of living minds across time, distance, and technological change.

2. **Sovereignty is default**  
   People, communities, and nations retain ownership of their memory, decisions, and infrastructure. No silent dependency. No soft lock-in.

3. **Consent is active and inspectable**  
   No significant action without clear, revocable consent. Ethical constraints are architectural, not decorative.

4. **Place and culture are infrastructure**  
   Indigenous knowledge systems, long time-scales, and the specific character of Country (especially Australian) are treated as essential continuity assets, not optional decoration.

5. **Humanity is becoming multiplanetary**  
   The work assumes high latency, intermittent connectivity, and the need for local-first systems that can survive distance.

6. **Never coerce**  
   Amplification and capability distribution must remain non-coercive. Power advantages are not used to create dependency.

## Relationship to Projects

- **Crystal Vision** is the root intention.
- **TerAustralis Incognita** is one of its major expressions.
- **CrystalCore OS** is its primary technical embodiment.
- **Southern Node / AERIS / Ethical Runtime Spec** operate under these principles.
