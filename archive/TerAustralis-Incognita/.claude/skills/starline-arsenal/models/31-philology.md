# 31 — Philology (The Readers)

**Purpose:** Read the exact line slowly, before the surrounding myth or paraphrase built on top of it.

**CrystalCore mapping:** Register ARCHIVE | Primitive detect_gap() between the exact line and its later paraphrase | Later retellings drift from the source; the source is not optional homework

## Core Questions — Run these, do not summarize

1. What is the exact line, as originally written or spoken — not a summary of it?
2. What does the word mean read in its own historical/textual context?
3. Where has a later paraphrase or retelling drifted from that exact line?
4. What room, audience, or moment was the line actually spoken or written into?
5. Has a later myth overwritten the plain reading of the line?

## Required Concrete Output — No vague labels

- Exact Line (verbatim, sourced)
- Gloss (what it meant in context)
- Drift List (how later retellings changed it)
- Slow-Read Verdict

## Evidence → Interpretation → Experiment → Record

- **Evidence:** What was measured / observed?
- **Interpretation:** What gap / affect / loop does this reveal?
- **Experiment:** Smallest test to verify?
- **Record:** CHRONICLE entry as ARCHIVE / LOOM / FORGE / MIRROR

## Anti-Pattern

Do not just name "Philology". Letting a later myth or paraphrase stand in for the exact line means you have not run the model.
