# 37 — Change Management (The Infrastructure Engines)

**Purpose:** Move an organization or system from state A to state B while keeping it functional.

**CrystalCore mapping:** Register FORGE + LOOM | Primitive transition() + anchor() | Continuous operation through discontinuity

## Core Questions — Run these, do not summarize

1. Who benefits from the old state, and who is made uncomfortable by the new one?
2. What work, relationships, or identity will people lose in this transition?
3. What is the smallest win that proves the new state is better?
4. How do we run both states in parallel during transition (dual operation)?
5. Who are the opinion-shapers, and do they have a path to success in the new state?

## Required Concrete Output — No vague labels

- Stakeholder Map (who wins | who loses | who is neutral | opinion-shapers)
- Transition Plan (phases, what runs in parallel, what must be sequential, go/no-go gates)
- Resistance Audit (what specific objections will surface, and what response to each)
- Success Story (the narrative that makes the new state feel inevitable, not threatening)

## Evidence → Interpretation → Experiment → Record

- **Evidence:** What has happened when this organization changed before?
- **Interpretation:** Who has real power to block or accelerate this, and why?
- **Experiment:** Pilot the new state in a low-stakes pocket; measure adoption friction.
- **Record:** CHRONICLE entry as FORGE (intended change) + LOOM (actual adoption curve)

## Anti-Pattern

Do not assume communication alone creates buy-in. Do not hide the losses people will experience. Do not move too fast and break trust; do not move too slow and lose momentum.
