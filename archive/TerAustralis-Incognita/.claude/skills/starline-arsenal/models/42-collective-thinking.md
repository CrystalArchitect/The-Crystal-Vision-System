# 42 — Collective Thinking (The Infrastructure Engines)

**Purpose:** How do groups of minds amplify or interfere with each other? Team cognition.

**CrystalCore mapping:** Register LOOM + ARCHIVE | Primitive map() + amplify() | Group coherence made visible

## Core Questions — Run these, do not summarize

1. Who thinks well together, and who creates friction despite competence?
2. Where does the group amplify individual insight vs. where does it dilute?
3. What unspoken assumptions does the group share that no one questions?
4. How much does the group defer to hierarchy vs. to the best idea?
5. What silences one person and what gives voice to another?

## Required Concrete Output — No vague labels

- Interaction Pattern Map (who engages with whom | where flows happen | where blocks happen)
- Amplification Points (where the group makes thinking better than any individual's)
- Interference Audit (where personality, rank, or group think suppresses good signals)
- Voice Map (whose ideas surface, whose disappear, and is that matching actual competence)

## Evidence → Interpretation → Experiment → Record

- **Evidence:** What is the actual decision-quality of this group vs. individuals alone?
- **Interpretation:** Where is coherence vs. artificial consensus?
- **Experiment:** Change one interaction pattern (e.g., who speaks first, who decides) and measure decision quality.
- **Record:** CHRONICLE entry as LOOM (group structure) + ARCHIVE (decision quality)

## Anti-Pattern

Do not assume silence means agreement. Do not confuse hierarchy with expertise. Do not optimize for consensus if it means suppressing dissonance that could be valuable. Do not mistake team cohesion for team thinking.
