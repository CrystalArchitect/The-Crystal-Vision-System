## Starline Arsenal critique (run 2026-08-30)

Applying [Asymmetric Thinking](../../../.claude/skills/starline-arsenal/models/13-asymmetric.md),
[OODA Loop](../../../.claude/skills/starline-arsenal/models/22-ooda-loop.md), and
[Margin of Safety](../../../.claude/skills/starline-arsenal/models/24-margin-of-safety.md)
against the plan as given — not a rewrite, a stress test.

**80/20 — holds up.** The plan already names its own leverage point (the
Teacher's Note): #2 + #1 unlock the rest, and #8 (the Ledger) is the
cheapest, most-reused artifact of the eight. That's a real 80/20 map, not
a wishlist trying to do all 8 at once — the plan is already lean.

**Risk the plan doesn't state:**

1. **No stated fallback if Week 1-2 slips.** The whole 12-week sequence
   is described as depending on #2 and #1 landing first ("unlock
   everything"). Nothing says what happens to weeks 3-12 if either slips
   past week 2 — no buffer, no re-sequencing trigger.
2. **The Ledger's 90-day quota has zero slack.** "12 entries min in 90d"
   on a *weekly* cadence is ~12-13 opportunities total — missing two
   updates already breaks the stated minimum. The plan calls the Ledger
   the single highest-leverage item and gives it the thinnest margin of
   safety of the eight.
3. **Two of eight deliverables are live public demos of failure-handling
   mechanisms specifically** — #2's "2-min kill-switch demo failing
   closed" and #4's "Operator Control Demo: user can trigger kill/inspect/
   fork live." Both are live, public, and about what happens when
   something is told to stop. That's a correlated risk (same failure
   mode — infra/permissions/timing — could sink both) the plan doesn't
   name, and neither lists a fallback (e.g. a pre-recorded backup) if the
   live version breaks in front of an audience.
4. **OODA loop speed is uneven and that's mostly fine, with one gap.**
   The Ledger runs a weekly loop; everything else runs on a 2-6 week
   loop. That's a reasonable design (fast sensor, slow actuators) — but
   it means the Ledger is also the one item with no external forcing
   function of its own (no scheduled call, no live audience), making it
   the easiest of the eight to quietly let slide even though the plan
   itself says it's the one to protect if only one thing ships.

**What's already strong, worth naming rather than just critiquing:**
every success metric is externally verifiable ("verifiable by outsider,"
"one external person can read/run it," "readable by engineer outside
lore") — the plan already applies the Incognita Rule to itself without
being asked to.
