# Dampier / Karratha — Phase 1 Anchor

Project Brief · TerAustralis Incognita · May 2026

## Purpose

This brief describes the Dampier/Karratha region in Western Australia as the proposed first node of the TerAustralis Incognita concept. It is an early-stage outline meant to support discussion and a feasibility conversation — not a development proposal. Anything described here would depend on feasibility studies, regulatory processes and genuine consultation with Traditional Owners.

## Why Dampier/Karratha

The Dampier/Karratha region is proposed as the anchor site because it brings together a strong combination of existing advantages:

- Established deep-water port infrastructure with significant capacity
- Proximity to major LNG and natural gas resources suitable for propellant production
- Strong connection to the Pilbara's world-class critical minerals operations (lithium, rare earths, gallium)
- Existing industrial workforce and logistics infrastructure
- Geographic alignment with current Starship splashdown zones in the Indian Ocean
- More operational flexibility compared to the heavily congested iron ore port at Port Hedland

## Proposed Phase 1 Functions

As an early-stage concept, a Phase 1 hub at Dampier/Karratha could focus on:

- Critical minerals processing and value-adding (particularly gallium, rare earths, and battery materials)
- Propellant production and storage, leveraging regional gas resources
- Recovery and refurbishment infrastructure for reusable launch vehicles
- Research activity, including environmental, marine and cultural studies tied to the site

## Partnership with Traditional Owners

The Dampier/Karratha area is part of the traditional Country of the Ngarluma and Yindjibarndi peoples. No part of this concept could proceed without their leadership and consent.

Engagement would begin through the proper native title and representative-body channels, based on free, prior and informed consent, benefit-sharing, employment pathways, and genuine governance participation.

## Environmental Considerations

Any development would need to carefully address marine and coastal ecology, air and water quality, and cumulative impacts alongside existing industrial activity. Environmental assessment and approvals would be an early priority.

## The Priority Milestone: A Feasibility Study

The single most valuable next step is an independent feasibility study of the Dampier/Karratha region as a Phase 1 site. This study would test the core assumptions around energy supply, engineering feasibility, environmental constraints, cultural considerations, and economic viability.

## What Would Be Needed Next

- Early conversations with Traditional Owner representative bodies
- Input from experts in port operations, energy, aerospace, and minerals processing
- Engagement with Western Australian and Commonwealth agencies
- A defined scope and sponsor for a formal feasibility study

This brief is a concept outline only. It is intended to open a conversation, and is expected to change as it is tested by experts and by the Traditional Owners whose Country this is.
