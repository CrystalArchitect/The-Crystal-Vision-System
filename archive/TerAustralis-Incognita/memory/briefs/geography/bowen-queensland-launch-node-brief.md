# Bowen — Queensland Launch Node

Project Brief · TerAustralis Incognita · May 2026

## Purpose

This brief outlines Bowen in North Queensland as the proposed launch node for the TerAustralis Incognita concept. It is an early-stage document intended to support discussion and feasibility assessment.

## Why Bowen

Bowen is proposed as Queensland’s contribution to the national network because it offers several strategic advantages:

- Home to the Bowen Orbital Spaceport at Abbot Point — Australia’s first licensed commercial orbital launch facility
- Existing regulatory approvals and launch infrastructure already in place
- Geographic location suitable for a wide range of orbital inclinations
- Proximity to the Bowen Basin, one of Queensland’s major mining and industrial regions
- Established industrial workforce and port infrastructure
- Positive engagement already occurring with the Juru Traditional Owners

## Proposed Functions

As Queensland’s launch node, Bowen could focus on:

- Small to medium orbital launch operations
- Development and testing of Australian launch vehicles
- Satellite deployment and rideshare missions
- Ground segment and mission control support
- Workforce training and space industry development

## Partnership with Traditional Owners

The Bowen area is part of Juru Country. Any development would require genuine partnership with the Juru Traditional Owners through their native title and representative bodies. This includes free, prior and informed consent, benefit sharing, employment opportunities, and respect for cultural heritage.

## Environmental Considerations

The site sits near the Great Barrier Reef Marine Park. Any future expansion would require thorough environmental assessment, particularly regarding coastal and marine impacts, noise, and emissions.

## Next Steps

The priority action is to conduct a feasibility assessment in partnership with the Juru people, local industry, and existing spaceport operators to determine how Bowen can best contribute to Australia’s growing space capability within the TerAustralis framework.

This document is a concept outline only and is subject to change following consultation and detailed feasibility studies.
