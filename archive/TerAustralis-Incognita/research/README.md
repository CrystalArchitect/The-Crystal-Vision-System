# Research

Exploratory work — **not production software** (Repository principle 2).
Material here may be unfinished, speculative, or wrong; that's what the
folder is for. Nothing in `research/` ships, and promotion into `src/` is a
deliberate, reviewed act (with an ADR when structural).

Belt-Three labels apply here as everywhere: research notes state whether
they are Science (checkable), Story, or Vision — see
[`docs/governance/The-Incognita-Rule.md`](../docs/governance/The-Incognita-Rule.md).
