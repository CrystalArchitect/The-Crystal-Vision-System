## Sourcing Narrative: Onshore Australian Supply Chain

The six-component proof-of-concept suite flows through a single Australian onshore pipeline, sourced from independently real companies with published capabilities and confirmed engagement:

### Tier 1: Mineral Feedstock — Iluka Resources (ASX: ILU)

**Role:** Titanium mineral-sands provider.

Iluka Resources operates one of the world's largest mineral-sands mines (Jacinth-Ambrosia in Western Australia) and produces high-purity titanium feedstock. For the Small Council PoC, Iluka supplies:

- **Titanium-grade mineral concentrate** (suitable for metallurgical reduction into Ti-6Al-4V ingots)
- **Pathway validation** for onshore feedstock supply (sourcing narrative anchor: rare earths benefited from Iluka's existing wet-processing capability at one site; titanium is Iluka's primary product, not a secondary attribution)

**Components supplied:**
- Combustion chamber stock (HEA-POC-04A) — titanium alloy ingot
- Nozzle insert stock (HEA-POC-04B) — titanium alloy ingot

**Benefit to Iluka:**
- Downstream demand signal from aerospace and emerging-tech sectors
- Case study: first Small Council PoC participant; demonstrated onshore feedstock relevance to space/propulsion applications
- Co-development scoping call; no exclusivity language

**Timeline:** Weeks 1–2 (mineral pathway validation and stock provisioning)

### Tier 2: Pilot-Scale Processing — Australian Rare Earths Ltd (AR3) + ANSTO

**Role:** Materials processing and characterization (rare-earth doping, advanced ceramics, quantum/RF diamond synthesis).

Australian Rare Earths Ltd launched a pilot-scale rare-earth and specialty-metals facility in June 2026, in partnership with Australia's Nuclear Science and Technology Organisation (ANSTO). The facility is the first in Australia capable of:

- **Rare-earth element processing** (REE extraction, purification, doping)
- **Specialty ceramic synthesis** (ceramic matrix composite precursors with REE dopants)
- **CVD diamond growth** (including nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center doping for quantum applications)

For the Small Council PoC, AR3 + ANSTO supply three material classes:

1. **Titanium alloying with rare-earth additions** (for strength/weight at high temperature)
   - Rare-earth doped Ti-6Al-4V ingot → HEA-POC-04A/B chamber and nozzle stock
   
2. **Ceramic matrix composite (CMC) thermal liner** (for extreme-temperature protection)
   - Rare-earth doped SiC/SiC CMC pre-impregnated tape
   - CVD-deposited bonding layers
   - Dimensional tuning post-sintering → HEA-POC-04C thermal liner

3. **CVD diamond quantum/RF components** (three precision parts)
   - NV-center resonator (diamond with nitrogen-vacancy color centers for quantum timing) → DFR-POC-02
   - RF window (low-loss diamond for satellite uplink/downlink) → DRF-POC-03
   - Thermal spreader (high-thermal-conductivity diamond for heat dissipation) → DTS-POC-01

All three diamond components are grown at AR3/ANSTO with:
- **Controlled nitrogen doping** (for NV centers in the resonator)
- **Thermal conductivity measurement** (spreader: target >5000 W/m·K)
- **RF loss characterization** (window: target <0.1 dB across 2–12 GHz)
- **Q-factor measurement** (resonator: target Q >10,000 for atomic-clock-grade stability)

**Benefit to AR3 + ANSTO:**
- First real customer pilot in quantum materials (NV-center diamond) AND advanced ceramics (CMC)
- Multi-domain capability proof: processes rare-earth metals, advanced ceramics, and quantum materials from the same facility
- Positions the June 2026 facility as an anchor for aerospace metallurgy, thermal ceramics, and emerging quantum/RF tech simultaneously
- Case study: demonstrated onshore capability across three material classes in a single PoC

**Timeline:** Weeks 2–7 (material synthesis, doping, growth, and characterization; all run in parallel)

### Tier 3: Integration and Assembly — Liquid Instruments (NRFC funded)

**Role:** Precision engineering, assembly, and systems integration.

Liquid Instruments, an Australian deep-tech company, received AU$28.45 million in NRFC (National Reconstruction Fund Corporation) funding in Sept 2026 to scale onshore precision engineering and advanced manufacturing. They are explicitly named by the Australian Space Agency as a critical capability for space-qualified hardware.

For the Small Council PoC, Liquid Instruments supply:

1. **Machining and structural assembly**
   - CNC machining of combustion chamber body (HEA-POC-04A) from AR3/ANSTO Ti-6Al-4V ingot
   - CNC machining of nozzle insert (HEA-POC-04B) from AR3/ANSTO Ti-6Al-4V ingot
   - Precision weld/braze for chamber-nozzle integration (pressure-vessel-qualified joints)

2. **Ceramic thermal-liner integration**
   - Dimensioning CMC liner (HEA-POC-04C) to fit inside chamber
   - Bonding CMC liner to chamber interior (braze or mechanical locking)
   - Thermal-stress verification (finite-element analysis)

3. **Diamond optical/RF finishing**
   - Polishing thermal spreader (DTS-POC-01) to >5000 W/m·K effective conductivity (measure via transient thermal response)
   - RF window (DRF-POC-03) optical finishing for <0.1 dB insertion loss (S-parameter measurement)
   - Resonator Q-factor tuning (DFR-POC-02) via surface refinement (resonance Q measurement)

4. **Engine assembly and integration testing**
   - Assembly of chamber + liner + nozzle as a single three-part system (not individual components)
   - Dimensional inspection (confirm tolerances, clearances, thermal-expansion alignment)
   - Pressure-jacket assembly (if applicable)
   - Preparation for thermal/flow testing (boundary condition mapping)

**Benefit to Liquid Instruments:**
- Case study spanning complete **engine assembly** (not just component machining) plus quantum/RF/communications tech
- Demonstrates capability across:
  - Pressure-vessel structures (aerospace-grade welding, materials-property control)
  - Ceramic bonding and integration (advanced thermal management)
  - Precision optical/RF finishing (emerging quantum and communications tech)
- Positions company as a complete-systems integrator, leveraging NRFC investment across both propulsion and quantum/RF frontiers
- Government briefing capability (NRFC visibility, Space Agency alignment)

**Timeline:** Weeks 6–13 (machining, assembly, finishing, and integration testing; overlaps with material synthesis to compress schedule)

## Tunneling Physics — Vision Framework

The Small Council PoC adopts a metaphorical framework from quantum tunneling to conceptualize barrier transcendence. See [`quantum-tunneling-barrier.md`](../mythos/teraustralis/vision/quantum-tunneling-barrier.md) for full methodology.

**Key metric: Transmission coefficient for network resonance across the barrier**

Under the specified model parameters (V₀ = 7.50 eV, E = 1.05 eV, L = 0.65 nm):

```
T ≈ 8.686e-8
```

This coefficient is computed as:
```
T = 1 / (1 + (V₀² sinh²(κL)) / (4EV₀(V₀ − E)))
```

Where κ ≈ 1.3011e10 m⁻¹, sinh(κL) ≈ 2354.71, and sinh²(κL) ≈ 5.5447e6.

**Interpretation (Vision layer, not measurement):** In Small Council's architectural metaphor, a 1 in ~11.5 million probability represents the odds that a signal can coherently traverse the latency barrier when both nodes are in resonance. The six-component suite serves as a **resonance anchor**: each material and each diamond component is characterized for its coherence properties (thermal conductivity, RF loss, resonator Q-factor), so that the full engine assembly can be tuned to maximize transmission probability across the barrier.

**Status:** This is a **design principle**, not a physics measurement. It provides narrative structure for why each component is chosen and measured.
