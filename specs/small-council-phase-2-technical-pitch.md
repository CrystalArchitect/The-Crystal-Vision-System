## Executive Summary

We propose a collaborative proof-of-concept demonstration: **an integrated suite of six space-qualified components—advanced materials and a complete high-heat engine assembly—sourced and manufactured through an entirely Australian onshore supply chain.**

This is not a partnership pitch or ownership deal. It is a co-development proposal: each company contributes its existing capability (rare-earths processing, precision manufacturing, advanced materials synthesis, supply coordination) to prove that the chain works end-to-end across two critical domains (electronics/quantum/timing and propulsion). The result is working hardware (three diamond components plus a three-part engine assembly — chamber, nozzle, and thermal liner) and a real-world case study in onshore sovereign capability spanning technology upgrade and propulsion pathways.

The three companies and their roles are already real. This proposal connects them through a single integrated supply pipeline.

## The Components: Six-Part Proof-of-Concept Suite

### Component 1: CVD Doped Diamond Thermal Spreader (DTS-POC-01)

**Application:** Satellite power electronics heat dissipation  
**Specifications:**
- Material: CVD diamond, rare-earth doped for thermal conductivity enhancement
- Size: ~50mm × 50mm × 2mm (integrated heat spreader for RF power amplifier module)
- Thermal target: 5000 W/m·K effective conductivity; heat transport from 150W amplifier to satellite frame
- Integration: Direct mount to GaAs or GaN RF transistor die; interface to satellite thermal bus

**Purpose in PoC:** Demonstrates rare-earth doping in advanced diamond synthesis; validates ANSTO capability for materials beyond traditional semiconductors.

---

### Component 2: CVD Doped Diamond Frequency Resonator (DFR-POC-02)

**Application:** Atomic clock or quantum timing standard  
**Specifications:**
- Material: CVD diamond, nitrogen-vacancy (NV) center doped for spin coherence
- Size: ~10mm cube (clock resonator cavity)
- Performance target: Q-factor >10,000; frequency stability <1 ppb (parts per billion) at X-band
- Quantum property: NV centers maintain coherence >1 millisecond (enabling atomic clock reference)

**Purpose in PoC:** Demonstrates diamond as quantum material; validates processing of NV-center doping at scale; positions Australia in quantum timing/sensing technology.

---

### Component 3: CVD Doped Diamond RF Window (DRF-POC-03)

**Application:** Satellite communications (X-band / Ku-band)  
**Specifications:**
- Material: CVD diamond, rare-earth doped for RF transparency
- Size: ~30mm diameter × 3mm thickness (vacuum window for RF waveguide)
- Performance target: <0.1 dB insertion loss at 12 GHz; vacuum-rated (10^-6 torr); thermal cycling certified -40°C to +60°C
- Purpose: Isolate satellite RF cavity from cryogenic cooler while maintaining signal integrity

**Purpose in PoC:** Demonstrates diamond RF performance; validates high-frequency window fabrication; fills gap in Australian space RF capability.

---

### Component 4: High-Heat Engine Assembly — Combustion Chamber (HEA-POC-04A)

**Application:** Lunar lander descent engine or orbital transfer stage main engine  
**Specifications:**
- Material: Grade 5 titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V) with rare-earth doped internal coating for thermal barrier performance
- Size: ~120mm length × 90mm OD × 60mm ID (sub-scale combustion chamber section)
- Thermal target: Sustained operation at 1500–1800K chamber wall temperature under combustion loading
- Performance requirements:
  - Pressure containment to 20 bar chamber pressure (sub-scale test pressure)
  - Regenerative or ablative cooling channel provision (test-article simplified)
  - Weld/joint integrity at injector interface

**Purpose in PoC:** Demonstrates the full high-heat combustion environment, not just a downstream nozzle section — proves the supply chain can deliver pressure-vessel-grade thermal structures.

---

### Component 5: High-Heat Engine Assembly — Nozzle Insert (HEA-POC-04B, formerly RENI-POC-04)

**Application:** Lunar lander descent engine or orbital transfer stage attitude thruster  
**Specifications:**
- Material: Grade 5 titanium alloy (Ti-6Al-4V), rare-earth doped for thermal performance
- Size: ~80mm length × 40mm OD × 15mm ID (nozzle exit/throat section)
- Thermal target: Stable operation at 1500K peak chamber temperature
- Performance requirements:
  - Erosion resistance to combustion products (LOx/methane or LOx/LH2)
  - Dimensional tolerance ±0.5mm (critical for nozzle flow characteristics)
  - Flight-heritage certification pathway (test-article qualified)

**Purpose in PoC:** Demonstrates supply chain capability for propulsion-critical components; validates rare-earth doping in thermal structures; represents traditional space-qualified manufacturing.

---

### Component 6: High-Heat Engine Assembly — Thermal Protection Liner (HEA-POC-04C)

**Application:** Chamber/nozzle interface thermal barrier, protecting structural titanium from direct combustion exposure  
**Specifications:**
- Material: Rare-earth doped ceramic matrix composite (CMC) liner, ANSTO-processed
- Size: ~100mm length, conforming sleeve fit inside chamber/nozzle bore
- Thermal target: Withstand 2000K+ transient exposure while limiting structural wall temperature to 1500K
- Performance requirements:
  - Thermal conductivity gradient characterization (hot-face to cold-face)
  - Bond integrity to titanium substrate under thermal cycling
  - Post-test inspection for spallation or delamination

**Purpose in PoC:** Completes the engine assembly as a real three-part system (chamber + liner + nozzle) rather than an isolated part; demonstrates rare-earth doped ceramics as a distinct materials capability alongside CVD diamond and titanium alloying.

## Why This Six-Part Suite

- **Supply chain breadth:** Proposed sourcing narrative (Iluka titanium feedstocks → ANSTO/AR3 processing → Liquid Instruments precision engineering) — framing only, not an existing partnership supports advanced materials (diamond), ceramics (thermal liner), and structural metals (chamber, nozzle) — proving supply chain flexibility across four distinct materials classes
- **Technology spectrum:** Diamond components represent satellite/quantum/electronics; the three-part engine assembly represents complete propulsion hardware — together, they span "technology upgrades and propulsion" pathways end to end
- **Real engineering challenges:** Each component requires all three companies to solve a distinct problem:
  - Diamond synthesis (ANSTO specialty)
  - Quantum/RF materials (downstream titanium / materials applications)
  - Ceramic matrix composite processing (ANSTO/AR3, thermal liner)
  - Pressure-vessel and precision thermal machining (Liquid Instruments core capability)
- **Space relevance:** All six components have direct flight applications (not terrestrial or academic); the engine assembly is a genuine sub-scale system, not a single isolated part
- **Timeline fit:** Feasible in 3–4 months in parallel (diamond synthesis in weeks 2–6; chamber/nozzle/liner machining in weeks 6–13; full engine assembly integration in weeks 12–13)
- **Demonstration value:** Six finished, tested components — three diamond parts and a complete three-part engine assembly — prove supply chain resilience, technology range, and sovereign manufacturing capacity in ways a report cannot

## What This Is Not

- **Not a liability claim.** No company is committing to production volumes, customer support, or exclusivity.
- **Not a partnership merger or equity deal.** Companies maintain independence; this is a time-limited co-development with clear exit points.
- **Not a regulatory process.** We are not lodging with Standards Australia or seeking government certification in Phase 2.
- **Not funding-seeking.** This proposal does not ask for new investment; it uses existing capability and demonstrates ROI through case study value.
- **Not an exclusive arrangement.** Each company can pursue parallel projects; this is a 13-week focused sprint.
