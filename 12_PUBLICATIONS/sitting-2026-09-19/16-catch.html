0. Status

This brief defines catch as industrial work on this continent: recover, refurbish, fuel. It does not select a berth. It does not inherit a pad. It does not offer a partnership, joint venture, or sponsored programme to any launch operator.

Pilbara / Port Hedland is not the proposed industrial coast on this sitting. That orientation is withdrawn (26 August 2026). A gigawatt-class methalox load is not an ordinary customer of the covered NWIS. A berth, if one is ever named, follows existing gigawatts and a crane, not the ore. Names of other organisations appear only as citations of public instruments or public architecture.

1. Definition

Cadence without a catch is only speed. A returning vehicle that cannot be inspected, recertified, and loaded again is a throwaway with extra steps.

Catch, here, is not a film still of mechanical arms. It is three jobs sharing one geography.

4. Proposed southern catch

A southern catch is geography plus plant:

  • a coast with a deep-water port and heavy industry already present
  • methane and oxygen that can be made and stored
  • a workforce that can inspect steel that has been to space
  • consent that starts with Traditional Owners if a place is named
  • electrical supply in the gigawatt class, not a mining-network leftover

No industrial coast is nominated. Follow existing gigawatts and a heavy-lift port. Do not collapse a sea tower with a methalox works. Not selected.

Sonic boom, debris, downrange safety, and environmental law are the body of catch. This brief does not copy environmental waivers written for other dirt. No is a complete answer.

5. Consent (binding)

Any named coast on this continent carries cultural heritage under custodianship of Traditional Owners and native title holders. No site selection, survey, construction, or catch-related works described here proceeds over an identified sacred site or songline path without Free, Prior and Informed Consent.

Legal floor, not ceiling. These are different machines:

  • Native Title Act 1993 (Cth) — recognition, not a grant of freehold. The “right to negotiate” is not FPIC. Minerals are usually Crown.
  • Aboriginal Heritage Act 1972 (WA) — heritage, not title. Restored 15 November 2023 after repeal of the 2021 Act.
  • Aboriginal Land Rights (Northern Territory) Act 1976 — inalienable freehold; land-council consent for leases.
  • Aboriginal Land Rights Act 1983 (NSW) — LALC compensation freehold, distinct from native title. Sydney axis: Document 06.

This brief does not identify or describe any specific sacred site or songline. “Streamline Indigenous consultations” is not the policy of this brief.

Juukan Gorge (May 2020) remains the named warning: acting within the then-legal minimum was not an adequate standard. Document 06 holds the same floor. The Pilbara is named in that paragraph as history, not as the nominated plant.
