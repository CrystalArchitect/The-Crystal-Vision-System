**Status:** direct, written permission from the copyright holder to a named
party. It sits alongside the public
[CC BY-NC-ND 4.0](../../LICENSE-CONTENT.md) licence on the mythos art; it does
not replace or alter that licence for anyone else.

**Not legal advice.** This is a plain-language grant written to record an
agreement clearly. Money changes hands under it, so both parties should have it

## Why this exists

The mythos art in [`mythos/art/`](../../mythos/art/) is published under CC
BY-NC-ND 4.0, which permits **neither commercial use nor derivatives**.
Inscribing the works as Ordinals and offering them for sale is commercial use,
and arguably derivative. Without this grant those acts would be unlicensed.

This document makes the permission explicit, so the inscriptions are properly
authorised — and so the public licence stays unchanged for everyone else.

## What is granted

A **non-exclusive, worldwide, revocable** permission to:

1. **Inscribe** the Covered Works as Ordinals on the BSV blockchain.
2. **Offer, sell and transfer** those inscriptions, retaining the proceeds.
3. **Reproduce** each Covered Work only as far as necessary to do the above —
   format conversion, resizing, inscription packaging.

## Covered Works

All images in [`mythos/art/`](../../mythos/art/) as at the date of this grant,
**except**:

- `breaker-of-the-e.jpeg`
- `lantern-and-the-breaker.jpeg`

Those two bear a real person's photographic likeness and carry a truth label in
the [art README](../../mythos/art/README.md) for that reason. They are outside
this grant and must not be inscribed or sold without that person's own consent.

Art added to `mythos/art/` after the date below is **not** covered
automatically; extending the grant takes a dated amendment to this file.

## Conditions

1. **Attribution.** Each inscription and listing credits *Crystal Arena-Turner —
   TerAustralis Incognita*.
2. **Honest provenance.** Where the art README records a work as AI-generated,
   listings must say so. No presenting generated work as hand-drawn.
3. **No misrepresentation.** Listings must not state or imply that the
   TerAustralis Incognita project issues a token, runs a sale, endorses an
   investment, or that purchase confers any right in the project, its software,
   its mythos, or its governance. **The project issues no token.** These are
   inscriptions of artwork, sold by a third party under licence, and nothing
   more.
4. **No sublicensing.** The Grantee may not pass this permission to anyone else.
5. **No name or trademark rights.** Nothing here grants rights in the names
   TerAustralis Incognita, CrystalCore, Incognita Lattice, Clementine, Starline or
   Dreamline beyond naming the source of the art.
6. **Art only.** The mythos text, code, documentation and governance material
   are outside this grant entirely.

## Revocation

The Grantor may revoke this grant at any time, in writing, for **future**
inscriptions and listings.

Revocation does not unwind inscriptions already made or sales already completed.
A blockchain inscription cannot be recalled, and both parties acknowledge that
before anything is inscribed.

## What this does not change

The public CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 licence on the mythos art is unaffected. No one other
than the named Grantee gains any commercial or derivative right by this
document, and the Grantor's copyright is not transferred, shared or diminished.
