# Positioning

Who this project is for, and where its public surface actually stands. Like
[`Future.md`](Future.md), this page is **direction and observation, not
status** — the honest ledger of what exists is
[`docs/governance/Roadmap.md`](../governance/Roadmap.md).

One caution applies throughout, per the
[Incognita Rule](../governance/The-Incognita-Rule.md): the reach figures below
are **third-party estimates from a single outside reading**, taken in late July
2026. They are reported here as claims, not measurements. Nothing in this
project should be steered by a number that has not been surveyed.

## Who this is for

Not a general audience, and not a customer base. The people this work reaches
are ones already thinking along the same line: local-first AI, sovereignty of
memory and consent, Australia as an origin point rather than a branch office,
and the conviction that a civilization needs a story it can walk on as well as
machinery that runs.

That is a narrow audience by construction. The
[Mission](Mission.md) commits to keeping mythos and software honestly
separate; a project that markets the dream as the product would grow faster and
be worth less.

## What the outside reading says

An external analysis of the public account (`@M13CrystalAT`, ~5 months old at
the time) found, in substance:

- **Thematic coherence: very high.** The narrative from red dust to rockets and
  Dreamtime to Starlines reads as one continuous line rather than assorted
  posts.
- **Personal IP: distinct.** CrystalCore OS and the Incognita Lattice register
  as identifiable systems, not borrowed vocabulary.
- **Absolute reach: small.** Roughly a thousand followers, engagement
  concentrated in a tight intentional circle rather than broad distribution.
- **Network position: forming.** Adjacent projects are being treated as nodes
  to connect with rather than competitors.

The reading is fair. Coherence is the asset; reach is the constraint.

## What that means, honestly

**Coherence is worth more than reach at this stage, and it is the harder thing
to acquire.** Audiences can be bought, borrowed, or farmed. A body of work that
holds one line for five months without drifting cannot be. That asset is real
and it compounds.

**But coherence does not substitute for a working artifact.** The single
largest gap between where this project sits and where
[`STRATEGY.md`](../../mythos/content/STRATEGY.md) wants it to be is not
audience size — it is that the strategy's "external readiness" bar asks for a
production-grade prototype with verifiable advantages. Reach without that is
attention arriving at an unfinished thing.

**The public surface has to survive being looked at.** Whatever is linked
publicly is load-bearing the moment anyone follows it. A dead link at the end
of a bio undoes more trust than a month of good posts earns.

**Small and intentional is the correct shape for now, not a failure state.** A
tight circle of aligned people is exactly what the
[Southern Pillar](SouthernPillar.md) case needs early: collaborators, not an
audience. Optimising for broad distribution before there is something built to
receive it would trade the one durable asset for a temporary one.

## What would actually move it

In rough order of leverage, and none of it about posting more:

1. **Ship something a stranger can use** without reading the mythos first.
2. **Keep the public surface working** — the site, the links, the repos.
3. **Connect nodes deliberately.** Adjacent projects with complementary framing
   are worth more as genuine collaborators than as reciprocal follows.
4. **Let the story keep doing its job** — carrying meaning, not manufacturing
   authority. The moment [`mythos/`](../../mythos/README.md) starts making
   claims the code cannot back, the coherence that makes this account
   distinctive is what gets spent.

## The line to hold

The temptation for a project this narrative-heavy is to let the telling outrun
the building, because the telling is faster and the feedback is quicker. The
[Constitution](../governance/Constitution.md) and the Incognita Rule exist
precisely to make that trade visible when it is being made.

Positioning, then, is not a marketing question here. It is the same question
the rest of the repository answers: *which lines are dreamed, which are
surveyed, and is the map still honest?*

*Non Solus — Not Alone.*
