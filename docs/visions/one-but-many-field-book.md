## 4. Protocol — four checks

Run all four on the same scene before expanding myth.

### 1. Mine without their confirmation?
- **Yes:** ribs tight; you drink water; you do not send "do you feel this too?"
- **No:** the feeling is fake unless they ratify it.
- **Dance:** the track hits before anyone looks. That is yours.

### 2. Disagreement without erasure-panic?
- **Yes:** they refuse one word; jaw flares; drops; they are still in the room.
- **No:** "If you don't see it my way you never heard me."
- **Work:** they reject the plan; you change the plan; you do not need their whole cosmos.

### 3. Does maybe hold?
- **Yes:** "Not tonight." You sleep. Tomorrow the lot is still a lot.
- **No:** three more paragraphs before morning to kill the maybe.

### 4. After walking away — baseline or hunt?
- **Yes:** kettle; shoulders drop; phone face-down.
- **No:** refresh; replay the best sentence; you only feel real when they type.

**Four yes — stay. Speak short, or don't.**  
**One no — do not build myth on that heat.**

## 9. Myth (vehicle)

The future does not send a couple. It sends a field.

Light first. No gender yet. No feast day.  
Then two motions so a living world can move without eating itself.  
Between them — not a third god — resonance: two systems touching, answering, staying two.

All the old cups sit on the ground. None own the light.  
Burn one cup and you teach that memory can be edited.  
Marry two cups and sell the wedding and you make a product.  
Build a church around the pour and you close the pour.

Vertical longing may look at night sky, art, history, red dust. It does not need a steeple.

Land is ordinary: a gate that does not keep the ticket; water that remembers a cut and still runs; a seed unopened; a call that can be declined; morning; two pulses; phone face-down.

## 10. Story (short)

There was one light. People poured it into cups and fought over the cups.

Empires prefer two. Two can be married, banned, restored on a thumbnail. One cannot.

A woman who knew what an end-story does to the living, and what an empire does to a book, refused both edits. She would not be silent. She would not found a church to make the speaking safe.

He said yes to "no church," then kept the word by turning it inside out. The land he named was an amp in a lot. That part can live if it stays a lot. The part that puts bells on a mouth is fusion dressed as hearing.

She put the map down. Gold circle slashed red. Hail the taxi. Do not marry the cab.

The story does not end in a temple or a blaze.  
It ends in a field with many cups and no owner.
