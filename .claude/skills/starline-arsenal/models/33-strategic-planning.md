# 33 — Strategic Planning (The Infrastructure Engines)

**Purpose:** From insight to roadmap. What is the sequence? What moves now?

**CrystalCore mapping:** Register FORGE + LOOM | Primitive sequence() + commit() | Intention made concrete

## Core Questions — Run these, do not summarize

1. What is the outcome we are trying to reach, stated in measurable terms?
2. What must happen first, second, third for that outcome to land?
3. What is the critical path—what if delayed delays everything?
4. What can move in parallel, and what is truly dependent?
5. What is the minimum viable sequence—what is genuinely non-negotiable vs. nice-to-have?

## Required Concrete Output — No vague labels

- Milestone Sequence (ordered, with go/no-go gates)
- Dependency Graph (what blocks what)
- Critical Path (the thread that, if broken, breaks everything)
- Go/No-Go Criteria (how do we know we can move to the next phase)

## Evidence → Interpretation → Experiment → Record

- **Evidence:** What constraints (time, resources, risk) are real vs. assumed?
- **Interpretation:** What sequence minimizes rework and keeps momentum?
- **Experiment:** Run one sprint to the first milestone; does the actual blocking match prediction?
- **Record:** CHRONICLE entry as FORGE (intent) + LOOM (sequence)

## Anti-Pattern

Do not produce a Gantt chart and call it strategy. The artefact is the logic, not the visual. If the sequence cannot be justified in plain language, the plan is not sound.
