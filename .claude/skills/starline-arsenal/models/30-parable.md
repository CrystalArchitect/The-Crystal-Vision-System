# 30 — Parable Thinking (The Readers)

**Purpose:** Make a lesson land through a short human story with an open ending, rather than a stated moral. Not a fable. Not an allegory. No borrowed lyrics — write original content, never quoted song or poem text passed off as the parable.

**CrystalCore mapping:** Register FORGE | Primitive close_with() an open story rather than a stated moral | A parable persuades by leaving room to arrive at the point yourself

## Core Questions — Run these, do not summarize

1. What is the surface scene — concrete, human, small?
2. What is the hidden lesson underneath it, left unstated?
3. What open question does the story leave the listener with, instead of a resolution?
4. Does this stay a story, or has it become a fable/allegory with the moral spelled out?
5. Is any real, identifiable person being cast as "the type" in the story? (Never do this.)

## Required Concrete Output — No vague labels

- Surface Scene
- Hidden Lesson (unstated in the telling)
- Open Question
- Forbidden Close (the moral-statement ending to avoid)

## Evidence → Interpretation → Experiment → Record

- **Evidence:** What was measured / observed?
- **Interpretation:** What gap / affect / loop does this reveal?
- **Experiment:** Smallest test to verify?
- **Record:** CHRONICLE entry as ARCHIVE / LOOM / FORGE / MIRROR

## Anti-Pattern

Do not just name "Parable Thinking". Preaching the moral outright, casting a real living person as the story's type, or borrowing lyrics/text from an existing song or poem instead of writing original content, means you have not run the model.
