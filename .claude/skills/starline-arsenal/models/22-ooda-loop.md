# 22 — OODA Loop (The Adaptors)

**Purpose:** Observe, Orient, Decide, Act — faster and tighter than whatever you're up against.

**CrystalCore mapping:** Register LOOM + FORGE | Primitive detect_gap() + close_with() in a tightening decision loop | A slow loop loses to a fast one even with better information

## Core Questions — Run these, do not summarize

1. What are we actually observing right now, unfiltered by our existing model?
2. How does this update our orientation — the mental model we're deciding from?
3. What is the decision that orientation actually points to?
4. What is the smallest action that tests the decision and feeds new observation back in?
5. Where is our loop slower than the thing we're responding to?

## Required Concrete Output — No vague labels

- Observe/Orient/Decide/Act Snapshot
- Orientation-Update Statement
- Action Taken
- Loop-Speed Comparison

## Evidence → Interpretation → Experiment → Record

- **Evidence:** What was measured / observed?
- **Interpretation:** What gap / affect / loop does this reveal?
- **Experiment:** Smallest test to verify?
- **Record:** CHRONICLE entry as ARCHIVE / LOOM / FORGE / MIRROR
