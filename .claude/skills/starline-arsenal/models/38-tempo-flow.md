# 38 — Tempo & Flow State (The Infrastructure Engines)

**Purpose:** What rhythm allows sustainable high-quality output? Speed vs. quality trade-off.

**CrystalCore mapping:** Register ARCHIVE + FORGE | Primitive measure() + sustain() | Rhythm made observable

## Core Questions — Run these, do not summarize

1. What is the natural pace at which this work produces its best output?
2. Where does rushing degrade quality faster than it saves time?
3. Where does slowing down (ritual, review, reflection) actually accelerate total progress?
4. What interruptions shatter focus, and which ones are unavoidable?
5. How long can this team run at the chosen pace before exhaustion or decay sets in?

## Required Concrete Output — No vague labels

- Tempo Audit (current pace | optimal pace | gap | cost of gap)
- Flow Condition Checklist (focus time | context switches | decision latency | threshold for "good enough")
- Quality-Speed Trade-off Map (what breaks at each speed increment)
- Sustainability Map (how long at current pace until burnout or backslide)

## Evidence → Interpretation → Experiment → Record

- **Evidence:** What is the actual throughput at the current pace? Quality metrics?
- **Interpretation:** Is the current pace sustainable, or are we borrowing from tomorrow?
- **Experiment:** Run one sprint at a different tempo (slower or faster); measure output and morale.
- **Record:** CHRONICLE entry as ARCHIVE (current state) + FORGE (intended rhythm)

## Anti-Pattern

Do not confuse "moving faster" with "working harder." Do not ignore early warning signs of unsustainable pace (errors, frustration, attrition). Do not mistake heroic work for efficiency.
