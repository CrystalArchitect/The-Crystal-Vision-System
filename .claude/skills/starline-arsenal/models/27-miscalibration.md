# 27 — Miscalibration (The Predictors)

**Purpose:** Separate the measured score, the guessed rank, and the method that sorted someone into a bucket — before a famous-effect label gets stamped on a person.

**CrystalCore mapping:** Register ARCHIVE | Primitive track() score vs guess vs method | A famous effect is not a character; score, guess, and method must each be named or the label does not apply

## Core Questions — Run these, do not summarize

1. What was the actual score?
2. What was the guessed rank or self-assessment?
3. What method sorted the person or claim into a bucket ("low", "high")?
4. Could random, noise-only data produce this same graph?
5. Is the famous-effect name being used as a measurement, or as a club?

## Required Concrete Output — No vague labels

- Score vs Guess vs Method
- Artefact Risk (low / medium / high — could noise alone explain this?)
- Keep-or-Drop Call on the label

## Evidence → Interpretation → Experiment → Record

- **Evidence:** What was measured / observed?
- **Interpretation:** What gap / affect / loop does this reveal?
- **Experiment:** Smallest test to verify?
- **Record:** CHRONICLE entry as ARCHIVE / LOOM / FORGE / MIRROR

## Anti-Pattern

Do not just point at confidence and name a famous effect — that is representativeness (see Heuristic Thinking), not measurement. Produce the score/guess/method artefact above, or do not use the label at all.
