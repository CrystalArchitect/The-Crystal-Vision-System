Design grammar

What makes the set cohere. Anything added later should obey these or it will read as a different pack.

  • Three materials, no moreWood, crystal, silver. Gold appears once, on the coin. Nothing else enters the set.
  • Crystal is structural, not decorativeIt forms brackets, hoops and bindings — it does the job metal would do in a conventional pack. That inversion is the pack’s signature.
  • Corner brackets plus rivetsEight bracket points and paired silver rivets recur on crate, chest, chair and books.
  • One latch designThe same silver clasp appears on crate, barrel, chest and every book in the stack.
  • Octagonal plinthSmall items — potion, gem, heart, scrolls — stand on a shared silver base so they sit at a common height.
  • Soft bevels throughoutNo hard edges anywhere. Every corner is rounded, which is what keeps the set readable at icon size.
  • Fixed cameraConsistent three-quarter view, single key light upper-left, soft contact shadow. Only the lantern deviates, and only because it hangs.

Before you use these

  • These are renders, not modelsFlat images from a generator. There is no mesh, no UV map and no rig behind any of them. Using them in an engine means either treating them as 2D sprites or modelling from them.
  • Every file carries a Grok watermarkBottom-right of all fifteen. It must be removed or cropped before any shipping use.
  • No alpha channelThe backdrop is baked in, and it is a radial gradient rather than a flat fill — corner samples range from #bb305b to #d54783. A single chroma key will leave a halo; these need per-asset matting.
  • Resolution is 1408 × 1408Ample for icons, short of what a hero or promotional render would want.
  • Check your generator’s termsCommercial use of generated imagery depends on the terms in force for the account that made it. Worth confirming before these reach a storefront.
  • Gaps in the setNo armour, no food, no ore or crafting material, no map/quest item, no negative-state icon (poison, curse). A second pass of six or so would close the common inventory categories.
