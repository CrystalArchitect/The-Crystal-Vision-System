The Silent Signal — Concept / Card

THE SILENT SIGNAL

Unanswered questions, distant intelligence, absence, listening, uncertainty, patience, the temptation to turn silence into certainty.

Epistemic Principle
Silence is not proof of absence.
But neither is silence proof of presence.

That becomes one of the Atlas's most important epistemic principles. It stops two common errors: filling the gap too quickly with a comforting story, and filling it too quickly with a dismissive denial. Both collapse the Unknown prematurely.

For readings: Silent Signal is not a yes or no. It is a card that says: you are listening at the edge. Stay in listening. Do not turn absence of signal into a story yet.
