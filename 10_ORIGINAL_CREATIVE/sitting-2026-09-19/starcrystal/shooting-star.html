Why This Belongs In The Atlas Differently

Fermi's Silent Line was a love song to the future — to someone not yet met. Shooting Star Girl is a love song to the present tense — to someone who already tore through your sky and left a burning trail. One is about waiting. The other is about impact.

In Atlas terms: Fermi is the Sovereign Gap. Shooting Star Girl is the Falling Star.

Falling Star / Wandering Star Vocabulary

In your Star Map vocabulary you already defined:

Falling Stars — sudden change
Wandering Stars — uncertainty
Double Stars — partnership / duality
Falling Star: Sudden, bright, impossible to ignore. Changes the whole sky in seconds. You don't need a rocket, don't need a crown — whole universe bends every time you come around.
Wandering Star: Does not stay on a fixed path. Tearing through sky, never looked back. Unpredictable, wild and unafraid.
Crystal Principle: Every time you break, you light another flame — fracture as illumination, not destruction. Principle III: Breaking is not always destruction. A fracture can reveal interior structure.
Garnet / Fire: Burning up the dark, reckless heart, kiss me even if we burn. Garnet is desire, courage, action, transformation. This is Garnet energy embodied as a person.

As Storysong

This IS a Storysong in its purest Atlas definition: a memory that exists simultaneously as story and music, a story remembered through feeling.

Story: Girl who is made of shooting stars, lights up whole world, hurricane, heavens turning
Song: You have it as melody already — anthemic chorus, whole damn sky falling for your spark
Feeling: Reckless heart, not caring where we start or end, wanting the burn

Atlas question it asks: What memory still burns even after it has passed?

Possible Atlas Embodiments

Option 1 — The Falling Star as Major Arcana variation: Between XVI Shattering and XVII Seven Sisters, a card of incandescent arrival that changes everything.

Option 2 — Garnet Court: The Lens or Prism of Garnet — the figure who burns, who cannot be contained.
Option 3 — Dream Map location: The Girl Made Of Shooting Stars as inhabitant of Glass Forest or Moon Garden — a magnetic dream that keeps attracting dreamer back.
Option 4 — Keep it outside deck as personal myth: Not every song needs to be a card. Some songs are the reason you built the map in the first place. Atlas remembers traveller, traveller changes map — Crystal, wild and unafraid is both subject and cartographer.

Law It Gives You

Some memories don't orbit. They fall through. They break and in breaking, light another flame.

You don't have to explain the high. You just keep chasing it through every hurricane.
