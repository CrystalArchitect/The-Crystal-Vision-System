Where It Lives In Atlas

Not a card for querent to draw lightly. A card that says: something thought buried is still guarded. Wire keeps memories on guard. Ghost pulls files from deep.

- Something thought buried is returning because conditions changed, not because you failed
- Proof in the black — you now have an outside measure that keeps it honest
- Task is not to re-bury, but to move it from Buried → Echoing → Bright, at a pace where high-trust is slower and that's the stronger pitch

Its law: No more shadows, no more chains — but also, no more carrying alone. Ghost and wire are a double star — partnership, duality — memory needs a second witness to become proof.
