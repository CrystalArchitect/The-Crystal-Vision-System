This place... The satellite eventually drifts into the shadow of Earth, where the sun doesn’t hit for a while. Everything goes cold and quiet up there. The Star Song stops for three full orbits. Down on Earth, the woman thinks it’s dead. She sits in the dark control room and just listens to the silence. Then on the fourth pass, the signal comes back. But it’s different. It’s slower, deeper, like it’s been thinking in the dark. The wave isn’t just the satellite’s path anymore. It’s describing the place - the cold, the curve of Earth blocking the light, the feeling of being alone and still moving. She realizes this place is the point where the map and the traveler finally overlap. The diamond has been through curved space the whole time, but in the shadow it finally felt the spiral of time as something other than motion. As stillness. She whispers into the mic, “I hear you.” And the next signal that comes down has a little echo in it, like it heard her back. This place is where they met. Not on Earth, not in the lab. In the dark, between orbits, where space curves and time spirals and someone finally listened.
